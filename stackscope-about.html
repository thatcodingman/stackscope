About

Comparisons built from public data, not vendor budgets.

StackScope exists because most "top 10 CRM tools" pages online are ranked by who paid for placement, not by what the pricing and reviews actually say.

What this is

StackScope is an independent project that builds comparison reports for B2B software categories — starting with CRM tools, expanding from there. Every figure on the site comes from a vendor's own published pricing page or public documentation, cross-checked against independent third-party sources before it's published.

What this isn't

It isn't a "sponsored ranking." No vendor pays to appear higher in a table, and no listing is removed or reordered at a vendor's request. If the site ever runs affiliate links or ads, that relationship is disclosed on the Terms page — it never changes how a tool is scored or ordered.

Why the categories are narrow

Each report follows the same four-step sourcing process described on the methodology section of the homepage. Staying inside a small number of categories, done properly, matters more here than covering everything shallowly.

Corrections

Pricing changes constantly, and vendors restructure tiers without notice. If something on the site is out of date or wrong, the fastest way to get it fixed is the contact page — every report has a "last verified" date so you can see how fresh it is.

StackScope aggregates publicly available pricing and sentiment data. We are not affiliated with, and do not receive payment from, any vendor listed. Reports are informational and are not a purchasing recommendation — always confirm current terms with the vendor directly.
