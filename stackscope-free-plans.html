FAQ

Common questions

Which CRM has the best free plan?

Per StackScope's CRM report, HubSpot's free plan covers 2 users and 1,000 contacts, and Zoho CRM's covers 3 users and 5,000 total records. Both are ongoing, not trials.

Which email marketing tool has the biggest free plan?

Kit's free plan covers 10,000 subscribers, with unlimited broadcasts, forms and landing pages, but only 1 basic automation and 1 email sequence.

Is Wave accounting really free?

Wave's Starter plan is $0 and includes unlimited estimates, invoices, bills and bookkeeping records. Online payments, receipt scanning and payroll are separate paid add-ons.

Which help desk has a free plan?

Help Scout has an ongoing free plan for up to 5 users, with 100 contacts per month and 1 inbox. Zendesk has no free plan, only a 14-day trial.
