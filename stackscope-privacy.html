Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. What this covers

This policy explains what information StackScope collects when you visit the site or use the contact form, and how it's used.

2. Contact form data

If you use the contact form, the name, email address, and message you submit are sent to StackScope via Netlify Forms so we can respond to you. This data is used only to address your message and isn't sold or shared with third parties.

3. Analytics

StackScope may use an analytics service (such as Google Analytics) to understand aggregate traffic patterns — for example, which reports are read most. This data is anonymized/aggregated where possible and is not used to identify individual visitors.

4. Advertising cookies

If the site displays third-party advertising (including through Google AdSense), the ad provider may use cookies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other sites. You can opt out of personalized advertising through your browser settings or via Google's Ads Settings.

5. Cookies, generally

Cookies may be used for basic site functionality and, where enabled, analytics and advertising as described above. You can disable cookies in your browser, though some site features may not work as expected.

6. Third-party links

Comparison reports link to third-party vendor websites. StackScope isn't responsible for the privacy practices of those external sites.

7. Children's privacy

StackScope is a B2B software resource and isn't directed at children. We don't knowingly collect information from children.

8. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. Material changes will be reflected in the "last updated" date above.

9. Contact

Privacy questions can be sent to hello@usestackscope.com or through the contact page.

StackScope aggregates publicly available pricing and sentiment data. We are not affiliated with, and do not receive payment from, any vendor listed. Reports are informational and are not a purchasing recommendation — always confirm current terms with the vendor directly.
