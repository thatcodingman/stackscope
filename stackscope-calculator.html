FAQ

Common questions

How does this calculator work?

It multiplies each tool's published entry-tier price by your team size, using the figures in StackScope's reports, last verified October 2026. It uses list prices, with the minimums and per-employee rules each vendor publishes, and does not include add-ons, AI usage, taxes or promotions.

Why do some tools show a higher price than the sticker rate?

Some vendors set minimums. Monday.com requires at least 3 seats on every paid plan, so a team of 1 or 2 pays for 3. Framer includes the first editor in its site fee and charges $20 per month for each additional Full Editor.

Is this a quote?

No. It is an estimate of entry-tier list prices. Always confirm current terms and your own needs with the vendor before buying.
