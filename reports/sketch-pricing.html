Sketch pricing 2026

This page covers Sketch's published prices, free plan limits and the catches, using StackScope's design tools report, last verified October 2026.

Sketch has no free editing plan, only a 30-day trial, though viewers are free and unlimited even without a subscription. Standard is $12 per editor per month on annual billing ($14 billed monthly), which is $60/month for 5 editors ($70 monthly) and $240/month for 20 editors ($280 monthly), with 50GB of storage per editor and unlimited documents. The main catch is the macOS limitation: full editing requires the Mac app, a real platform constraint for mixed-OS teams, although a one-time Mac-only perpetual license is also available as an alternative to the subscription. Sketch is praised for a clean, Mac-native interface and design-system workflow.

FAQ

Common questions

How much does Sketch cost?

Standard is $12 per editor per month on annual billing ($14 billed monthly): $60/month for 5 editors and $240/month for 20 editors on annual billing.

Does Sketch have a free plan?

No editing plan. There is a 30-day trial only, but viewers are free and unlimited without a subscription.

Does Sketch work on Windows?

Full editing requires the Mac app, which is a real constraint for mixed-OS teams. A one-time Mac-only perpetual license is also available.
