Zendesk vs Freshdesk pricing

Zendesk and Freshdesk are two of the most common help desk choices for small and mid-size support teams. This page compares their free plans, tier prices and AI terms using StackScope's help desk report, last verified October 2026.

The two vendors list the same price at the entry tier ($19 per agent) and the mid tier ($55 per agent). The real difference is the free plan: Freshdesk has an ongoing free plan for up to 2 agents, while Zendesk has no free plan, only a 14-day trial with no card required. At the top published tier, Freshdesk Enterprise is listed at $89 per agent against $115 for Zendesk Suite Professional, though Zendesk's Enterprise is sales-led and not publicly posted. Zendesk's AI agents are billed per automated resolution beyond a limited monthly allowance, and its Copilot is a separate $50 per agent per month.

FAQ

Common questions

Is Zendesk or Freshdesk cheaper?

At the entry tier and mid tier they list the same per-agent price: $19 and $55. At the highest public price Freshdesk Enterprise is $89 per agent versus $115 for Zendesk Suite Professional, although Zendesk's own Enterprise tier is sales-led and not publicly posted.

Does Zendesk or Freshdesk have a free plan?

Freshdesk has an ongoing free plan for up to 2 agents, covering basic ticketing, a knowledge base and reporting. Zendesk has no free plan, only a 14-day free trial with no card required.

How does Zendesk charge for AI?

Zendesk's AI Agents are included but billed per automated resolution beyond a limited monthly allowance, at $1.50 per committed resolution or $2.00 pay-as-you-go. Copilot is a separate $50 per agent per month.
