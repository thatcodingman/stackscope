Dropbox Sign vs DocuSign pricing

Dropbox Sign and DocuSign both have a single-user entry plan and a pricier team tier. This page compares their free plans, entry prices and team pricing using StackScope's e-signature report, last verified October 2026.

For one person the two are almost identical in price: Dropbox Sign Essentials is $10.05 per month annual-equivalent ($15 billed monthly) with unlimited signature requests, and DocuSign Personal is $10 per month with 5 envelopes per month. For teams Dropbox Sign is cheaper: Standard is $17.50 per user per month on annual billing with a 2-user minimum, about $350 per month at 20 users, against $25 per user per month for DocuSign Standard, about $500 per month at 20 users. Dropbox Sign's free plan allows 3 signature requests per month and up to 3 signers per request. DocuSign's free plan is also 1 user only, and its send limit isn't exposed as one clean figure.

FAQ

Common questions

Is Dropbox Sign cheaper than DocuSign?

For teams, yes: Dropbox Sign Standard is $17.50 per user per month on annual billing (about $350 per month at 20 users) versus $25 per user per month for DocuSign Standard (about $500 at 20 users). For one person the prices are about the same, at $10.05 and $10 per month.

Do Dropbox Sign and DocuSign have free plans?

Yes, both ongoing. Dropbox Sign's allows 3 signature requests per month and up to 3 signers per request, without templates or team features. DocuSign's is a 1-user plan whose send limit isn't exposed as one clean vendor figure.

How many documents can I send on the entry plan?

Dropbox Sign Essentials includes unlimited signature requests. DocuSign Personal includes 5 envelopes per month.
