DocuSign pricing 2026

DocuSign's cheapest plan is built for one person, and teams move to a higher tier. This page covers Personal, the first team tier, and the envelope limits, using StackScope's e-signature report, last verified October 2026.

Personal is $10/month ($120 billed annually, the same whether billed annually or monthly) and is a 1-user plan with 5 envelopes per month. The first team-capable tier is Standard at $25 per user per month on annual billing, which comes to about $500/month at 20 users; Standard and Business Pro allow 100 envelopes per user per year. StackScope's report also lists an ongoing 1-user free plan, for 1 user only, whose send limit isn't exposed as one clean vendor figure; a third-party cross-check reports 3 sends per month. The main catch is that cost rises quickly once envelope allowances and higher-end agreement features matter, since advanced workflow and AI sit in the pricier IAM plans, not Personal. DocuSign is praised for an easy, familiar signing flow.

FAQ

Common questions

How much does DocuSign cost?

Personal is $10/month for 1 user ($120 billed annually). The first team-capable tier, Standard, is $25 per user per month on annual billing, about $500/month at 20 users.

Does DocuSign have a free plan?

Yes, an ongoing 1-user plan, not a team plan. Its send limit isn't exposed as one clean vendor figure; a third-party cross-check reports 3 sends per month.

How many envelopes does DocuSign allow?

Personal allows 5 envelopes per month. Standard and Business Pro allow 100 envelopes per user per year. Reaching the allowance doesn't make sending unlimited; it requires a higher plan or purchased capacity.
