Rippling pricing 2026

This page covers Rippling's published prices, free plan limits and the catches, using StackScope's payroll and hr software report, last verified October 2026.

Rippling has no permanent free plan, only a trial or promotion. Its live pricing page does not publish a self-serve payroll price and routes full configurations to a custom quote, and there is no named mid tier because modules such as benefits, time tracking and IT are priced separately. Rippling's own 2026 payroll and HRIS content cites reference figures of $35 per month plus $8 per employee per month, and Expense Management at $14 per employee per month as one module example, but StackScope does not extrapolate these into a posted price. Sources also conflict on whether Rippling offers a free trial: G2 says it does not, while Capterra labels it as having one. The main catch is that modular add-ons can raise cost and the breadth of features can create a learning curve.

FAQ

Common questions

How much does Rippling cost?

Rippling doesn't publish a self-serve payroll price; full configurations route to a custom quote. Its own 2026 content cites $35 per month plus $8 per employee as reference figures, but these are not presented as a posted price.

Does Rippling have a free trial?

Sources conflict: G2 says Rippling does not offer a free trial, while Capterra labels it as having one. There is no permanent free plan.

Why is Rippling's pricing hard to compare?

It is modular. Benefits, time tracking and IT are priced separately, and the module-level rate card is not fully public.
