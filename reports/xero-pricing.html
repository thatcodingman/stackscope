Xero pricing 2026

Xero raised its US prices on October 1, 2026. This page covers the new prices for all three published tiers, using StackScope's accounting report, last verified October 2026.

Effective October 1, 2026, Early is $27/month (up from $25), Growing is $59/month (up from $55) and Established is $97/month (up from $90). The increase applies to new and existing US subscribers and excludes add-ons and sales tax. There is a 30-day free trial but no ongoing free plan, and accountant and bookkeeper access is included at no extra cost. Capterra currently shows stale pre-increase figures, so StackScope uses the vendor's own pricing. The main catch, per G2 reviews, is advanced reporting and customization limits and occasional bank-feed issues. Xero is praised for a clean interface, ease of use, invoicing and reconciliation.
