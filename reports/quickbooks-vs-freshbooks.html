QuickBooks Online vs FreshBooks pricing

QuickBooks Online and FreshBooks are two of the most-searched accounting tools for small businesses. This page compares their entry prices and user limits using StackScope's accounting report, last verified October 2026.

FreshBooks has the cheaper entry tier: Lite is $23 per month against $38 for QuickBooks Simple Start. QuickBooks Simple Start covers 1 user plus 2 accountant accesses, while FreshBooks includes 1 user and charges $11 per month for each additional Team Member, which StackScope estimates at $67 per month for 5 users on Lite. QuickBooks Essentials is $85 per month for 3 users plus 2 accountants. Both carry promotion conflicts: Intuit advertises 50% off for 3 months, which would put Simple Start at $19, while its other pages show stale figures, and FreshBooks' page still shows 90% off despite offer terms saying it ended August 18, 2026. Neither has an ongoing free plan, only a 30-day trial.

FAQ

Common questions

Is FreshBooks cheaper than QuickBooks?

At the entry tier, yes: FreshBooks Lite is $23 per month versus $38 for QuickBooks Simple Start. With extra Team Members at $11 each, StackScope estimates FreshBooks at $67 per month for 5 users.

Do QuickBooks and FreshBooks have free plans?

Neither has an ongoing free plan. Both offer a 30-day free trial.

Can I rely on the promotional prices?

Not without checking. Intuit advertises 50% off for 3 months, which would put Simple Start at $19, but other Intuit pages show stale figures. FreshBooks' 90% off conflicts with its own offer terms, and StackScope has not resolved that.
