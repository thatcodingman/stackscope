Help Scout pricing 2026

This page covers Help Scout's published prices, free plan limits and the catches, using StackScope's help desk software report, last verified October 2026.

Help Scout's free plan allows up to 5 users, 100 contacts per month and 1 inbox, and is ongoing rather than a trial. Paid tiers are Standard at $25 per user ($30 billed monthly), Plus at $45 ($54 monthly) and Pro at $75 ($90 monthly, with a 10-seat minimum). The main catch is that enterprise teams will outgrow it: it lacks advanced routing, deep SLA controls and reporting depth on the lower tiers. AI Answers, the chatbot resolution feature, is a separate $0.75-per-resolution charge after a three-month free trial. Help Scout is repeatedly praised for feeling like an organized, human email inbox rather than an enterprise ticketing system.
