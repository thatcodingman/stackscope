Xero vs FreshBooks pricing

Xero and FreshBooks both target small businesses, and Xero raised its prices on October 1, 2026. This page compares their published tiers using StackScope's accounting report, last verified October 2026.

FreshBooks is cheaper at every matched tier on standard monthly prices: Lite is $23 per month against $27 for Xero Early, Plus is $43 against $59 for Xero Growing, and Premium is $70 against $97 for Xero Established. The tiers differ in what limits them, though: FreshBooks Plus covers 50 billable clients and Premium is unlimited, and paid team-member add-ons can raise the real cost as a business grows, while Xero includes accountant and bookkeeper access at no extra cost. Neither has an ongoing free plan, only a 30-day trial. FreshBooks' pricing page still shows a 90%-off promotion that its own offer terms previously said ended August 18, 2026, and StackScope has not resolved that conflict.

FAQ

Common questions

Is FreshBooks cheaper than Xero?

On standard monthly prices, yes: FreshBooks Lite is $23, Plus is $43 and Premium is $70, versus Xero's Early at $27, Growing at $59 and Established at $97 since October 1, 2026. FreshBooks tiers are limited by client count, so check the caps before comparing.

Do Xero and FreshBooks have free plans?

Neither has an ongoing free plan. Both offer a 30-day free trial.

Does FreshBooks limit clients?

Yes. Plus covers 50 billable clients and Premium is unlimited. Paid team-member add-ons can also raise the cost as a business grows.
