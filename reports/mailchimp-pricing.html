Vendor pricing

Mailchimp pricing 2026

Mailchimp prices by contact count, so the bill depends on list size. This page covers its free plan, the published Standard tiers, and the billing catches, using StackScope's email marketing report, last verified October 2026.

The free plan covers 250 contacts and 500 sends per month, with 1 seat and 1 audience. It caps sending at 250 per day, and sending pauses if you exceed it rather than triggering a charge. Standard is $17/month at 500 contacts ($20 billed monthly), $51/month at 2,500 contacts ($60 monthly) and $115/month at 10,000 contacts ($135 monthly); the vendor presents the 10,000-contact price as a first-year promotional rate for eligible businesses, not a guaranteed renewal rate. The main catch is that the bill scales fast with contact count and lower tiers cap automation, and G2 reviewers repeatedly flag "expensive" as a theme. Mailchimp is praised for a drag-and-drop builder that makes professional campaigns easy for non-technical users.

FAQ

Common questions

How much does Mailchimp cost?

Standard is $17/month at 500 contacts, $51/month at 2,500 contacts and $115/month at 10,000 contacts, billed annually. Monthly billing is $20, $60 and $135. The 10,000-contact price is presented as a first-year promotional rate for eligible businesses.

Does Mailchimp have a free plan?

Yes: 250 contacts and 500 sends per month, with 1 seat and 1 audience. Sending is capped at 250 per day, and pauses if exceeded rather than triggering a charge.

Why does Mailchimp get expensive?

The bill scales quickly with contact count, and lower tiers cap automation. G2 reviewers repeatedly flag "expensive" as a recurring theme.
