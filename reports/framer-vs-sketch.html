Framer vs Sketch pricing

Framer and Sketch are both used for web and interface design, with different pricing models. This page compares them using StackScope's design tools report, last verified October 2026.

For one editor Framer is cheaper: Basic is $10 per month on annual billing ($15 monthly) against $12 ($14 monthly) for Sketch Standard. For teams Sketch is cheaper. Using StackScope's arithmetic, 5 editors cost $60 per month on Sketch and $90 on Framer, and 20 editors cost $240 on Sketch and $390 on Framer, on annual billing. Framer charges $20 per month for each additional Full Editor after the first. Framer's free plan allows up to 3 editors but lacks a custom domain and production publishing, while Sketch offers only a 30-day trial. Sketch's catch is that full editing requires the Mac app.

FAQ

Common questions

Is Sketch cheaper than Framer?

For teams, yes: $60 versus $90 per month for 5 editors, and $240 versus $390 for 20, on annual billing. For one editor Framer Basic is $10 versus $12 for Sketch Standard.

Does Sketch work on Windows?

Full editing requires the Mac app, a real constraint for mixed-OS teams. Viewers are free and unlimited.

Do Framer and Sketch have free plans?

Framer's allows up to 3 editors without a custom domain or production publishing. Sketch offers only a 30-day trial.
