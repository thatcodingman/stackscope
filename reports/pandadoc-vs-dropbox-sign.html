PandaDoc vs Dropbox Sign pricing

PandaDoc is a document-workflow tool and Dropbox Sign is a simpler e-signature product. This page compares them using StackScope's e-signature report, last verified October 2026.

Dropbox Sign has the lower entry price: Essentials is $10.05 per month annual-equivalent ($15 billed monthly) for 1 user, against $19 ($35 monthly) for PandaDoc Starter. For teams the gap narrows: Dropbox Sign Standard is $17.50 per user per month on annual billing, about $350 per month at 20 users, while PandaDoc Starter is $380 at 20 users on annual billing ($700 monthly). Free plans differ too. PandaDoc's allows unlimited seats but only 60 documents per year and 2 recipients per document, while Dropbox Sign's allows 3 signature requests per month and up to 3 signers. PandaDoc gates some workflow features as add-ons, including External Automations at $39 per month on Business and AI Renewals at $20 per seat per month.

FAQ

Common questions

Is Dropbox Sign cheaper than PandaDoc?

Yes: Essentials is $10.05 per month versus $19 for PandaDoc Starter on annual billing. At 20 users, about $350 versus $380 per month.

What do the free plans include?

PandaDoc's: unlimited seats, 60 documents per year, 2 recipients per document. Dropbox Sign's: 3 signature requests per month, up to 3 signers per request.

What does PandaDoc charge extra for?

External Automations is a $39 per month per account add-on on Business, and AI Renewals is a $20 per seat per month add-on on Business.
