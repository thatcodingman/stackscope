Figma pricing 2026

This page covers Figma's published prices, free plan limits and the catches, using StackScope's design tools report, last verified October 2026.

Figma Professional is $16 per Full seat per month on annual billing ($20 billed monthly), which is $80/month for 5 editors ($100 monthly) and $320/month for 20 editors ($400 monthly). The free plan is ongoing, with 3 team files, unlimited personal drafts, 30-day version history, 500 AI credits per month and unlimited free Viewers; it lacks unlimited team files and advanced collaboration and admin features. Professional includes unlimited files and folders. The main catch is seat-role complexity: Full, Dev, Collab and View seats have materially different capabilities and prices, from $3 to $90 depending on plan and role. Figma is praised above all for real-time collaboration.
