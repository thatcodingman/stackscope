ClickUp vs Notion pricing

ClickUp and Notion are both flexible all-in-one workspaces, and ClickUp is cheaper on paper. This page compares them using StackScope's project management report, last verified October 2026.

ClickUp is cheaper at both paid tiers: Unlimited is $7 per member ($10 billed monthly) against $10 ($12 monthly) for Notion Plus, and Business is $12 ($19 monthly) against $20 ($24 monthly) for Notion Business. On free plans, ClickUp allows unlimited members but only 60MB of total shared storage, while Notion's is unlimited for individual use but caps collaborative workspaces at 1,000 blocks, with 5MB file uploads and 7-day page history. On AI, ClickUp's Brain add-on is $9 per member per month, while full Notion AI is exclusive to the $20 Business plan. StackScope notes that independent sources disagree on Notion's pricing, so confirm on Notion's site.

FAQ

Common questions

Is ClickUp cheaper than Notion?

At both paid tiers, yes: $7 versus $10 per member, and $12 versus $20, on annual billing. Billed monthly, $10 versus $12 and $19 versus $24.

Which has the better free plan?

They trade off. ClickUp has unlimited members but 60MB of total shared storage. Notion is unlimited for individual use but caps collaborative workspaces at 1,000 blocks.

How do ClickUp and Notion price AI?

ClickUp's Brain add-on is $9 per member per month. Full Notion AI requires the $20 Business plan.
