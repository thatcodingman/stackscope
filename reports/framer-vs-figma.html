Framer vs Figma pricing

Framer and Figma overlap on design and prototyping but are priced differently: Framer by site plus seats, Figma by seat. This page compares them using StackScope's design tools report, last verified October 2026.

For a single editor Framer is cheaper: Basic is $10 per month on annual billing ($15 billed monthly) with the first editor included, against $16 per month ($20 monthly) for a Figma Professional Full seat. With a team the order reverses. Additional Framer Full Editors are $20 per month each, which by StackScope's arithmetic comes to $90 per month for 5 editors and $390 for 20 on annual billing, against Figma's $80 and $320. Both have ongoing free plans: Framer's allows up to 3 editors but lacks a custom domain and production publishing, and Figma's includes 3 team files and unlimited free Viewers.

FAQ

Common questions

Is Framer cheaper than Figma?

For one editor, yes: Framer Basic is $10 per month on annual billing versus $16 for a Figma Professional Full seat. For 5 editors Framer comes to $90 per month versus Figma's $80, and for 20 editors $390 versus $320, on annual billing.

What are the limits of Framer's and Figma's free plans?

Framer's free plan allows up to 3 editors, 1,000 pages and 1GB of bandwidth, but has no custom domain or production publishing. Figma's includes 3 team files, unlimited personal drafts, 30-day version history and unlimited free Viewers.

Why is Framer's team cost higher than its headline price?

Framer prices by site, seat and usage. The $10 headline includes the first editor; each additional Full Editor is $20 per month and content editors are $10 per month.
