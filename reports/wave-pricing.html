Wave pricing 2026

This page covers Wave's published prices, free plan limits and the catches, using StackScope's accounting software report, last verified October 2026.

Wave's Starter plan is $0 and ongoing, with unlimited estimates, invoices, bills and bookkeeping records; online payments, receipt scanning and payroll are separate paid add-ons. Pro is $19 per month or $190 per year, priced per business profile rather than per owner or seat, and includes unlimited user access. A promotional $9.50 per month for the first 3 months is not the standard price. Wave presents Pro as its only paid subscription tier, so there is no mid tier to price. The main catch is that payment processing fees are where Wave's real cost lives: Starter charges 2.9% plus $0.60 per Visa, Mastercard or Discover transaction and 3.4% plus $0.60 for Amex, and Pro waives the fixed $0.60 fee for the first 10 qualifying transactions in each monthly subscription period before reverting to the same rate as Starter.
