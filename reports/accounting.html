FAQ

Common questions

Did Xero's prices change recently?

Yes — Xero raised its US standard monthly prices across all three tiers effective October 1, 2026: Early went from $25 to $27, Growing from $55 to $59, and Established from $90 to $97. This applies to both new and existing US subscribers.

Does QuickBooks Online have a Plus tier between Essentials and Advanced?

Yes — QuickBooks Online Plus sits between Essentials ($85/month, 3 users) and Advanced ($340/month, 25 users), priced at $140/month for 5 users. It adds inventory tracking, project profitability, and budgeting on top of Essentials' features.

Is Wave really free for core bookkeeping?

Yes — Wave's Starter plan is $0 on an ongoing basis and includes unlimited estimates, invoices, bills, and bookkeeping records with unlimited users. The real cost for most users comes from payment processing fees (2.9% + $0.60 per Visa/Mastercard/Discover transaction), not the subscription itself.

Does FreshBooks charge extra for additional team members?

Yes — every FreshBooks plan includes only 1 user by default. Additional Team Members cost $11/month each, so a 5-person team on the $23/month Lite plan would actually cost about $67/month ($23 + 4 × $11).
