Figures are aggregated from vendor pricing pages and independent third-party review research, verified August 22, 2026. Current promotional offers were checked where publicly displayed as of that date — see each vendor's cell for the verified offer status, since promotional terms can change without notice (Xero's own promo changed at least three times over the course of 2026 alone). Review sentiment for this report draws on G2 across all four tools, plus Capterra for FreshBooks specifically — it does not represent Trustpilot or Reddit consensus, which were not gathered for this report. No "Best value" badge is assigned in this report: the four tools have materially different user/capacity models (per-user fees vs. unlimited users, different invoice/client caps), and a price-only badge would misrepresent that. Confirm current pricing directly with each vendor before purchasing. This is not a recommendation to buy any specific product.

Promotional-price caution: FreshBooks is the clearest example of a current-page/offer-terms mismatch found during this research — its pricing page still displayed "90% off for 3 months" while FreshBooks' own formal offer terms stated that specific promotion ended August 18, 2026. This report does not treat that displayed promo as a verified active offer. Always check a vendor's offer terms page, not just the headline price, before assuming a promo is still running.
