Justworks vs Gusto pricing

Justworks and Gusto both publish payroll prices, which makes them easy to compare on cost for small teams. This page uses StackScope's payroll and HR report, last verified October 2026.

At the entry tier Gusto is cheaper: Simple is $49 per month plus $6 per person, which is $79 per month for 5 employees and $169 for 20, against Justworks Payroll at $8 per employee plus a $50 base, which is $90 per month for 5 employees and $210 for 20. Gusto's Simple tier is single-state only, while Justworks Payroll includes multi-state payroll and contractor payments, so the cheaper price comes with a capacity limit. Justworks also sells PEO plans: PEO Basic is $79 per employee per month and PEO Plus is $124 with benefits administration included. Neither has an ongoing free plan.

FAQ

Common questions

Is Gusto cheaper than Justworks?

At the entry tier, yes: Gusto Simple is $79 per month for 5 employees and $169 for 20, while Justworks Payroll is $90 and $210. Gusto Simple is single-state only, and Justworks Payroll includes multi-state payroll.

What is Justworks PEO pricing?

PEO Basic is $79 per employee per month and PEO Plus is $124 per employee per month, with benefits administration included. EOR is separate at $599 per employee per month.

Do Gusto and Justworks have free plans?

Neither has an ongoing free plan. Gusto's free period ends with the first payroll run, and Justworks advertises 3 months free for Payroll as a promotion.
