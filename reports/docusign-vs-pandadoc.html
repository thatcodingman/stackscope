DocuSign vs PandaDoc pricing

DocuSign and PandaDoc handle e-signatures very differently on their entry plans. This page compares their free plans, capacity limits and per-user prices using StackScope's e-signature report, last verified October 2026.

DocuSign's cheapest plan, Personal at $10/month, is a 1-user plan with 5 envelopes per month, so a team has to move to Standard at $25 per user per month on annual billing. PandaDoc Starter is $19 per user per month on annual billing ($35 monthly) with unlimited documents and eSignatures, and no monthly send cap. PandaDoc's free plan allows unlimited seats but only 60 documents a year and 2 recipients per document, which blocks sending until the next annual cycle unless you upgrade. DocuSign's Personal free plan is single-user only.

FAQ

Common questions

Is DocuSign or PandaDoc cheaper?

For one person, DocuSign Personal at $10 per month is cheaper than PandaDoc Starter at $19 per month on annual billing, but Personal is limited to 5 envelopes per month and one user. For teams, PandaDoc Starter is $19 per user per month versus $25 per user per month for DocuSign Standard, both on annual billing.

Do DocuSign and PandaDoc have free plans?

Yes, both are ongoing. DocuSign's free Personal plan is for 1 user only. PandaDoc's free plan has unlimited seats but is capped at 60 documents a year and 2 recipients per document.

Does PandaDoc limit how many documents I can send?

Not on Starter, which includes unlimited documents and eSignatures. The 60-documents-per-year cap applies only to the free plan.
