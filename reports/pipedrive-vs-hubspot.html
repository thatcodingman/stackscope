Pipedrive vs HubSpot pricing

Pipedrive and HubSpot are both popular with small sales teams, and their entry prices sit close together. This page compares their free plans, per-user prices on annual and monthly billing, and automation limits, using StackScope's CRM report, last verified October 2026.

The cheaper vendor depends on how you pay. On annual billing Pipedrive Lite is cheaper at every size measured: $14 vs. $20 per user for 1 user, $70 vs. $100 for 5 users and $280 vs. $400 for 20 users. On monthly billing the order flips, because Pipedrive Lite is $24, $120 and $480 while HubSpot Sales Hub Starter stays at $20, $100 and $400 whether billed annually or monthly. HubSpot also has an ongoing free plan for 2 users and 1,000 contacts, where Pipedrive offers only a 14-day trial. Pipedrive's Lite tier has no automations or sequences and no two-way email sync, which Growth adds.

FAQ

Common questions

Is Pipedrive cheaper than HubSpot?

On annual billing, yes: Pipedrive Lite is $14, $70 and $280 per month for 1, 5 and 20 users, versus $20, $100 and $400 for HubSpot Sales Hub Starter. On monthly billing HubSpot is cheaper, because Pipedrive Lite is $24, $120 and $480 while HubSpot's standard rate is the same on either billing basis.

Do Pipedrive and HubSpot have free plans?

HubSpot does: an ongoing free plan for 2 users and 1,000 contacts. Pipedrive has no free plan, only a 14-day trial.

What is missing from Pipedrive's cheapest tier?

Automations, sequences and two-way email sync. Pipedrive's Growth tier adds both.
