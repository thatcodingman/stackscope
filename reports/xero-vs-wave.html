Xero vs Wave pricing

Xero is a paid accounting platform and Wave leads with a free plan. This page compares them using StackScope's accounting report, last verified October 2026.

Wave is cheaper: its Starter plan is $0 and includes unlimited estimates, invoices, bills and bookkeeping records, and Wave Pro is $19 per month or $190 per year, against $27 per month for Xero Early since its October 1, 2026 price rise from $25. Xero has no ongoing free plan, only a 30-day trial, and Early is capped at 20 invoices and 5 bills. Xero states no per-user license fees and includes accountant and bookkeeper access at no extra cost. Wave Pro is priced per business profile with unlimited users. Wave's online payments, receipt scanning and payroll are separate paid add-ons. Xero's main US page advertises a first-3-months promotion, while other pages still show an older offer, so verify at checkout.

FAQ

Common questions

Is Wave cheaper than Xero?

Yes: Wave Starter is $0 and Wave Pro is $19 per month, against $27 per month for Xero Early since the October 1, 2026 increase.

Does Xero limit invoices?

Early is capped at 20 invoices and 5 bills. Wave Starter includes unlimited estimates, invoices and bills.

Do Xero and Wave charge per user?

Neither: Xero states no per-user license fees, and Wave Pro includes unlimited user access.
