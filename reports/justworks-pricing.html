Vendor pricing

Justworks pricing 2026

This page covers Justworks's published prices, free plan limits and the catches, using StackScope's payroll and hr software report, last verified October 2026.

Justworks has no ongoing free plan publicly listed; the pricing page advertises "3 Months Free!" for Payroll, which is a promotion rather than a permanent free tier. Payroll is $8 per employee per month plus a $50 base, which comes to $90 per month for 5 employees, $210 for 20 and $450 for 50. PEO Basic is an alternative model rather than a payroll upgrade, at $79 per employee per month ($395 per month for 5 employees and $1,580 for 20), and PEO Plus is $124 per employee per month with benefits administration included. EOR is separate at $599 per employee per month. Payroll includes multi-state payroll and contractor payments. The main catch is that reviewers cite limited reporting and customization and some benefits and pricing limitations.
