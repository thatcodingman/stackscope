Calendly vs SavvyCal pricing

Calendly and SavvyCal are priced identically per seat, so the difference comes down to the free plan and features. This page shows both side by side using StackScope's scheduling report, last verified October 2026.

The paid prices match: both are $10 per seat per month on annual billing ($12 billed monthly), which is $50 per month for 5 seats and $200 per month for 20 seats, though SavvyCal's monthly base rate is sourced from Capterra. The difference is the free plan. Calendly has an ongoing free plan for 1 user with 1 event type and 1 connected calendar, while SavvyCal has no ongoing free tier, only a free try and a 30-day money-back guarantee. Calendly gates advanced team and routing features above Standard, and SavvyCal is praised for its calendar-overlay experience, with G2 reviewers naming pricing as its main negative theme.

FAQ

Common questions

Is Calendly cheaper than SavvyCal?

No, the paid prices match: both are $10 per seat per month on annual billing ($12 billed monthly), or $50 and $200 per month for 5 and 20 seats. The difference is that Calendly has an ongoing free plan and SavvyCal doesn't.

Does SavvyCal have a free plan?

No ongoing free tier. The current vendor page offers a free try and a 30-day money-back guarantee. Calendly's free plan covers 1 user, 1 event type and 1 connected calendar.

What do reviewers like about SavvyCal?

The calendar-overlay experience, the user experience and flexible availability controls. G2 identifies pricing as the main negative theme in its small review sample.
