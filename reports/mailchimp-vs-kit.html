Mailchimp vs Kit pricing

Mailchimp and Kit are the two best-known email tools for small senders and creators. This page lines up their free plans and published tiers using StackScope's email marketing report, last verified October 2026.

Kit's free plan is far larger: 10,000 subscribers with unlimited broadcasts, forms and landing pages, against 250 contacts and 500 sends per month for Mailchimp. Kit's free plan deliberately withholds automation, offering only 1 basic automation and 1 email sequence. On paid plans the picture shifts with list size. Mailchimp Standard is $17 per month at 500 contacts, $51 at 2,500 and $115 at 10,000, though the vendor presents the 10,000-contact figure as a first-year promotional rate. Kit Creator is $33 at 1,000 subscribers, $49 at 3,000 and about $117 at 10,000, an annual-effective figure rounded from Kit's schedule. Mailchimp also bills for every contact in the audience, including unsubscribed ones, unless you archive them.

FAQ

Common questions

Is Kit cheaper than Mailchimp?

It depends on list size. Kit Creator is $49 per month at 3,000 subscribers, while Mailchimp Standard is $51 at 2,500 contacts. At 10,000 the figures are about $117 for Kit and $115 for Mailchimp, but Mailchimp's is a first-year promotional rate.

Which has the better free plan?

Kit's: up to 10,000 subscribers, without automation beyond 1 basic automation and 1 sequence. Mailchimp's covers 250 contacts and 500 sends per month.

Does Mailchimp charge for unsubscribed contacts?

Yes. It bills for every contact in your audience, including unsubscribed ones, unless you manually archive them.
