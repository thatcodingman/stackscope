Acuity Scheduling pricing 2026

This page covers Acuity Scheduling's published prices, free plan limits and the catches, using StackScope's scheduling software report, last verified October 2026.

Acuity has no ongoing free plan, only a 7-day trial that also restricts outbound communication. It is priced per account with a calendar allowance per tier rather than per seat: Starter is $16 per month on annual billing ($20 billed monthly) with 1 calendar, Standard is $27 per month ($34 monthly) with 6 calendars, and Premium is $49 per month ($61 monthly) with 36 calendars. Premium also includes the AI Booking Assistant at no extra charge, which is not available on Starter or Standard. At the calendar limit you move to a higher plan, with no overage fee identified. The main catch is that the entry plan's one-calendar limit forces an upgrade for multi-calendar teams. Reviews praise customization and integrations.

FAQ

Common questions

How much does Acuity Scheduling cost?

Starter is $16/month with 1 calendar, Standard is $27/month with 6 calendars and Premium is $49/month with 36 calendars, on annual billing. Monthly billing is $20, $34 and $61.

Does Acuity Scheduling have a free plan?

No. There is a 7-day trial only, and it restricts outbound communication.

How is Acuity priced?

Per account with a calendar limit per tier, not per seat. At the limit you move to a higher plan; no overage fee was identified.
