Cheapest at each size: Among the entry tiers that actually scale to each seat count, SignNow is cheapest at all three breakpoints ($8/mo at 1 user, $40 at 5, $160 at 20, annual billing) — but its unresolved envelope-cap conflict makes it the riskiest pick to commit to without confirming directly with SignNow first. Among tools with a genuinely unlimited-scaling team tier, PandaDoc is the clear second at $19/$95/$380. DocuSign and Dropbox Sign's headline prices ($10 and $10.05) are individual-only and can't be compared directly against the other two past 1 seat — their real team pricing (DocuSign Standard ~$25/user, Dropbox Standard ~$17.50/user) runs meaningfully higher than either SignNow or PandaDoc at scale.
