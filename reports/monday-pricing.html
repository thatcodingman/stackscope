Monday.com pricing 2026

Monday.com looks cheap per seat, but every paid plan has a seat minimum. This page covers its three tiers, the free plan's limits, and what the real minimum bill is, using StackScope's project management report, last verified October 2026.

Basic is $9 per seat ($12 billed monthly), Standard is $12 per seat ($14 monthly) and Pro is $19 per seat ($24 monthly). Every paid plan has a 3-seat minimum, so even a solo user pays for three seats, which puts the real minimum bill at $27 per month on Basic billed annually. The free plan allows up to 2 seats but only 3 boards, which StackScope's report describes as usable for a trial rather than real team work. Monday.com is praised for its highly visual, customizable boards.
