Calendly pricing 2026

Calendly has an ongoing free plan and a per-seat paid tier. This page covers what the free plan allows, what Standard costs at 1, 5 and 20 seats, and what is gated above it, using StackScope's scheduling report, last verified October 2026.

The free plan covers 1 user, 1 event type and 1 connected calendar, and is ongoing; unlimited event types and multiple calendars are paid. Standard is $10 per seat per month on annual billing ($12 monthly), which works out to $50/month for 5 seats and $200/month for 20 seats on annual billing ($60 and $240 monthly). No separate contact or booking-volume cap was identified in the pricing material reviewed. The main catch is that advanced team and routing features are gated above Standard, and reviewers repeatedly flag higher-tier gating and expense for them. Reviews consistently praise ease of use and calendar integration.

FAQ

Common questions

How much does Calendly cost?

Standard is $10 per seat per month on annual billing ($12 billed monthly): $50/month for 5 seats and $200/month for 20 seats annually, or $60 and $240 monthly.

Does Calendly have a free plan?

Yes, an ongoing plan for 1 user with 1 event type and 1 connected calendar. Unlimited event types and multiple calendars require a paid plan.

What features are gated above Calendly Standard?

Advanced team and routing features. Reviewers repeatedly flag higher-tier gating and expense for them.
