FAQ

Common questions

Does Freshdesk have a free plan?

Yes — Freshdesk's free plan supports up to 2 agents with basic ticketing, a knowledge base, and reporting, and it's an ongoing plan, not a trial. A separate 14-day all-access trial is also available for testing the paid tiers.

Which help desk tool is cheapest to start?

Zendesk and Freshdesk both start at $19/agent/month (billed annually). Freshdesk is generally the better value at that price point since it also offers a free 2-agent plan to start on, while Zendesk has no free plan at all.

How much do AI features cost on top of the base seat price?

It varies by tool and is usually separate from the seat price. Zendesk AI Agents bill $1.50–$2.00 per resolution beyond a limited allowance, plus a $50/agent/month Copilot add-on. Freshdesk's Freddy AI Agent includes 500 free sessions/month, then $49 per 100 additional sessions. Intercom's Fin bills $0.99 per outcome, plus a $29–35/agent/month Copilot add-on. Help Scout's AI Answers cost $0.75 per resolution after a three-month free trial.

Does Help Scout work for large support teams?

Not ideally. Help Scout is repeatedly praised for feeling like an organized, human email inbox rather than an enterprise ticketing system, but it lacks advanced routing, deep SLA controls, and reporting depth on its lower tiers — and its top Pro tier has a 10-seat minimum.
