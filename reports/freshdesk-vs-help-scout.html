Freshdesk vs Help Scout pricing

Freshdesk and Help Scout both have free plans and appeal to smaller teams. This page compares them using StackScope's help desk report, last verified October 2026.

Freshdesk has the cheaper entry tier: Growth is $19 per agent against $25 per user ($30 billed monthly) for Help Scout Standard. Higher up the order reverses, with Help Scout Plus at $45 ($54 monthly) against Freshdesk Pro at $55, and Help Scout Pro at $75 ($90 monthly, 10-seat minimum) against Freshdesk Enterprise at $89, though the tiers do not map one-to-one. Help Scout's free plan is larger, covering up to 5 users with 100 contacts per month and 1 inbox, against up to 2 agents for Freshdesk. Help Scout's AI Answers costs $0.75 per resolution after a three-month free trial, while Freshdesk's Freddy AI Agent includes 500 sessions per month and then charges $49 per 100 additional.

FAQ

Common questions

Is Freshdesk cheaper than Help Scout?

At the entry tier, yes: $19 per agent versus $25 per user. At the middle and top tiers Help Scout is lower ($45 and $75 versus $55 and $89), but the tiers don't map exactly.

Which has the bigger free plan?

Help Scout's: up to 5 users with 100 contacts per month and 1 inbox. Freshdesk's covers up to 2 agents.

How do they price AI?

Help Scout's AI Answers is $0.75 per resolution after a three-month free trial. Freshdesk's Freddy AI Agent includes 500 sessions per month, then $49 per 100 additional.
