Vendor pricing

YouCanBookMe pricing 2026

This page covers YouCanBookMe's published prices, free plan limits and the catches, using StackScope's scheduling software report, last verified October 2026.

YouCanBookMe's free plan is ongoing and includes 1 calendar connection and 1 booking page. Individual is $8.10 per month on annual billing ($9 billed monthly) with 2 calendar connections and booking pages, and Professional is $11.70 per month ($13 monthly) with 6. Both are flat account-priced plans rather than per-calendar rates. Teams switches to a per-member price: 20 members at $16.20 each comes to $324 per month on annual billing ($360 monthly, 20 times $18). The main catch is that the free and entry tiers constrain calendars and booking pages, while advanced customization takes more setup or higher tiers. Reviews praise ease of use and calendar integration.
