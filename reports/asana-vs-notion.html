Asana vs Notion pricing

Asana is built for task and project tracking, and Notion is an all-in-one workspace, but they compete for the same budgets. This page compares their prices using StackScope's project management report, last verified October 2026.

Notion is cheaper at both paid tiers: Plus is $10 per member ($12 billed monthly) against $10.99 ($13.49 monthly) for Asana Starter, and Business is $20 ($24 monthly) against $24.99 ($30.49 monthly) for Asana Advanced. The free plans work differently. Asana's is limited to 2 users with unlimited tasks and projects, while Notion's is unlimited for individual use but caps collaborative workspaces at 1,000 blocks. AI is where the bills diverge: Full Notion AI is exclusive to Business, while Asana caps AI at 5 requests per user per month on both Starter and Advanced. StackScope notes that independent pricing sources disagree with each other, and with Notion's own figures, so check Notion's page before buying.

FAQ

Common questions

Is Notion cheaper than Asana?

At both paid tiers, yes: Notion Plus is $10 per member versus $10.99 for Asana Starter, and Notion Business is $20 versus $24.99 for Asana Advanced, on the billing basis shown.

Which has the better free plan, Asana or Notion?

Asana's free plan allows 2 users with unlimited tasks and projects. Notion's is unlimited for individual use, but collaborative workspaces cap at 1,000 blocks.

How do Asana and Notion handle AI?

Full Notion AI requires the Business plan. Asana caps AI at 5 requests per user per month on both Starter and Advanced.
