Monday.com vs Notion pricing

Monday.com is a visual work-management tool and Notion is an all-in-one workspace, but teams often compare their seat prices. This page uses StackScope's project management report, last verified October 2026.

At the entry tier Monday.com is cheaper per seat, with Basic at $9 ($12 billed monthly) against $10 ($12 monthly) for Notion Plus. But every Monday.com paid plan has a 3-seat minimum, which puts the real minimum bill at $27 per month on Basic with annual billing, so Notion is cheaper for very small teams. At the upper tier Monday.com Pro is $19 ($24 monthly) against $20 ($24 monthly) for Notion Business. Monday.com's free plan is limited to 2 seats and 3 boards, while Notion's is unlimited for individual use with collaborative workspaces capped at 1,000 blocks. Full Notion AI requires the Business plan, while each Monday.com tier includes a fixed monthly AI-credit allowance with no published price for buying more.

FAQ

Common questions

Is Monday.com cheaper than Notion?

Per seat at the entry tier, yes: $9 versus $10 on annual billing. But Monday.com requires 3 seats on every paid plan, so the real minimum is $27 per month on Basic.

What are the free plan limits?

Monday.com's free plan covers up to 2 seats and 3 boards. Notion's is unlimited for individual use, with collaborative workspaces capped at 1,000 blocks.

Which includes AI?

Each Monday.com tier includes a fixed monthly AI-credit allowance, with no published price for more. Full Notion AI requires the $20 Business plan.
