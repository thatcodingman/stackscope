ADP vs Justworks pricing

ADP requires a quote for every tier, while Justworks publishes its payroll prices. This page compares what each shows, using StackScope's payroll and HR report, last verified October 2026.

Justworks Payroll is $8 per employee per month plus a $50 base, which is $90 per month for 5 employees, $210 for 20 and $450 for 50. ADP's Essential Payroll, Enhanced Payroll and HR Pro tiers have no public list price, and StackScope does not estimate one. Both advertise 3 months free as a promotion rather than a permanent free tier. Justworks also sells PEO Basic at $79 per employee per month, which bundles benefits access and is a separate model from Payroll. ADP's higher tiers add HR support, applicant tracking, learning management and harassment-prevention training.

Is ADP or Justworks cheaper?

Justworks Payroll is $90 per month for 5 employees and $210 for 20. ADP requires a quote, so StackScope can't compare directly.

Do ADP and Justworks have free plans?

Neither has a permanent free plan. Both advertise 3 months free as a promotion.

What does Justworks PEO include?

PEO Basic is $79 per employee per month and bundles benefits access. It's a separate co-employment model, not a payroll upgrade.
