Asana vs Monday.com pricing

Asana and Monday.com are two of the most popular project management tools, and both advertise a free plan. This page compares what each free plan actually allows, what each paid tier costs per seat, and the catches in each, using StackScope's project management report, last verified October 2026.

Monday.com's entry tier is cheaper per seat ($9 vs. $10.99 for Asana Starter on annual billing), but every Monday.com paid plan has a 3-seat minimum, so even a solo user pays at least $27/month on Basic. Both free plans cap at 2 users, but Monday.com's is limited to 3 boards, which the report calls usable for a trial rather than real team work, while Asana's free plan has unlimited tasks and projects. Asana also caps AI use even on paid tiers.

FAQ

Common questions

Is Asana or Monday.com cheaper?

Per seat, Monday.com Basic is $9 on annual billing versus $10.99 for Asana Starter. But Monday.com requires a minimum of 3 seats on any paid plan, so the smallest possible bill on Basic is $27 per month.

Which has the better free plan, Asana or Monday.com?

Both are limited to 2 users. Asana's free plan has unlimited tasks and projects, with advanced reporting, workload and approvals gated to paid tiers. Monday.com's free plan is limited to 3 boards.

What is Monday.com's 3-seat minimum?

Every paid Monday.com plan requires at least 3 seats, so even a solo user pays for three. On Basic billed annually that puts the real minimum bill at $27 per month.
