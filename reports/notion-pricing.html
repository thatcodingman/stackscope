Notion pricing 2026

This page covers Notion's published prices, free plan limits and the catches, using StackScope's project management software report, last verified October 2026.

Notion's free plan is unlimited for individual use, but collaborative workspaces with 2 or more members cap at 1,000 blocks, with 5MB file uploads, 7-day page history and 10 guest invites. Plus is $10 per member ($12 billed monthly) and Business is $20 per member ($24 monthly); Enterprise has no published price. The main catch is that full Notion AI is exclusive to the Business plan, while Free and Plus users get only a limited complimentary AI trial, and third-party listings don't agree on the price. Notion is praised for extreme flexibility as an all-in-one workspace, with docs, wikis and relational databases in one tool.
