FAQ

Common questions

Does Calendly have a free plan?

Yes — Calendly's Free plan is ongoing, supporting 1 user, 1 event type, and 1 connected calendar. Unlimited event types and multiple connected calendars both require a paid Standard plan ($10/month annual) or higher.

Is Acuity Scheduling priced per user?

No — Acuity is priced per account with calendar limits baked into each tier, not per seat. Starter includes 1 calendar, Standard 6 calendars, and Premium 36 calendars; a team needing more calendars moves up a tier rather than paying per additional user.

Does SavvyCal publish a monthly (non-annual) price?

Not directly — SavvyCal's own pricing page only shows the annual-billing view ($10 Basic / $17 Premium per seat/month). The commonly cited $12/$20 monthly figures come from Capterra, a third-party source, not SavvyCal's vendor page.

Is YouCanBookMe priced per calendar connection?

Only partly. Individual and Professional are flat, account-priced plans that include a set number of calendar connections and booking pages (2 and 6, respectively) — they aren't billed per calendar. Only the Teams tier, at $16.20/member/month annual, is genuinely priced per member.
