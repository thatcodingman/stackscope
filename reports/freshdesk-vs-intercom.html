Freshdesk vs Intercom pricing

Freshdesk and Intercom are priced per seat, but their AI charges work very differently. This page compares them using StackScope's help desk report, last verified October 2026.

Freshdesk is cheaper at every tier: Growth is $19 per agent, Pro is $55 and Enterprise is $89, against Intercom's Essential at $29 per seat ($39 billed monthly), Advanced at $85 ($99) and Expert at $132 ($139). Freshdesk's monthly USD prices are not publicly posted on its current pricing page, so the billing basis isn't directly comparable. Freshdesk also has an ongoing free plan for up to 2 agents, while Intercom offers only a 14-day trial. On AI, Freshdesk's Freddy AI Agent includes the first 500 sessions per month and then charges $49 per 100 additional sessions, with Copilot at $29 per agent per month on Pro and Enterprise. Intercom's Fin bills $0.99 per outcome on top of seats, and its Copilot adds $29 per agent per month billed annually.
