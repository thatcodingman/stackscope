Monday.com vs ClickUp pricing

Monday.com and ClickUp are two of the most-compared project management tools, and they price very differently at the bottom end. This page uses StackScope's project management report, last verified October 2026.

ClickUp is cheaper at the entry tier: Unlimited is $7 per member ($10 billed monthly) against $9 per seat ($12 monthly) for Monday.com Basic. Business is $12 per member on ClickUp, the same as Monday.com Standard on annual billing, but $19 versus $14 on monthly billing, so the order depends on how you pay and the tiers do not map exactly. Monday.com has a 3-seat minimum on every paid plan, which puts the real minimum bill at $27 per month on Basic with annual billing. On free plans, ClickUp allows unlimited members but only 60MB of total shared storage, while Monday.com's free plan is limited to 2 seats and 3 boards. On AI, ClickUp's Brain add-on is $9 per member per month, while each Monday.com tier includes a fixed monthly AI-credit allowance with no published price for buying more.

FAQ

Common questions

Is ClickUp cheaper than Monday.com?

At the entry tier, yes: ClickUp Unlimited is $7 per member versus $9 per seat for Monday.com Basic, on annual billing. At the middle tier the annual prices match at $12, but billed monthly ClickUp Business is $19 versus $14 for Monday.com Standard.

Does Monday.com have a seat minimum?

Yes. Every paid plan has a 3-seat minimum, so even a solo user pays for 3 seats, which is $27 per month on Basic with annual billing.

What are the free plan limits?

ClickUp's free plan has unlimited members but 60MB of total shared storage. Monday.com's allows up to 2 seats and 3 boards.
