Salesforce vs Pipedrive pricing

Salesforce and Pipedrive sit at opposite ends of CRM complexity. This page compares their entry prices and free options using StackScope's CRM report, last verified October 2026.

Pipedrive Lite is cheaper at every seat count measured. On annual billing it is $14, $70 and $280 per month for 1, 5 and 20 users, against $25, $125 and $500 for Salesforce Starter Suite, which is the same whether billed annually or monthly. Billed monthly, Pipedrive Lite rises to $24, $120 and $480, which narrows the gap to almost nothing at 1 user. Salesforce has an ongoing free plan for 2 users that requires logging in at least once every 60 days, while Pipedrive offers only a 14-day trial. Pipedrive's Lite tier has no automations or sequences, which Growth adds. Salesforce's catch is complexity and a learning curve, with cost escalating as you add customization.

FAQ

Common questions

Is Pipedrive cheaper than Salesforce?

Yes at every seat count measured. On annual billing, Pipedrive Lite is $14, $70 and $280 per month for 1, 5 and 20 users versus $25, $125 and $500 for Salesforce Starter Suite. Billed monthly, Pipedrive is $24, $120 and $480.

Do Salesforce and Pipedrive have free plans?

Salesforce has an ongoing free plan for 2 users, which requires logging in at least once every 60 days. Pipedrive has only a 14-day trial.

What does Pipedrive's cheapest tier leave out?

Automations and sequences, both of which Pipedrive's Growth tier adds.
