Zendesk pricing 2026

Zendesk is priced per agent, with no free plan. This page covers its published tiers, how its AI is billed, and where costs add up, using StackScope's help desk report, last verified October 2026.

Support Team is $19 per agent ($25 billed monthly), Suite Team is $55 per agent ($69 monthly), and Suite Professional is $115 per agent ($149 monthly). Enterprise is sales-led and not publicly posted. There is no free plan, only a 14-day free trial with no card required. The main catch is that add-ons add up fast. AI Agents are included but billed per automated resolution beyond a limited monthly allowance, at $1.50 per committed resolution or $2.00 pay-as-you-go, and Copilot is a separate $50 per agent per month. Zendesk is praised for deep omnichannel workflows, granular automation and reporting through Explore.

FAQ

Common questions

How much does Zendesk cost per agent?

Support Team is $19 per agent ($25 billed monthly), Suite Team is $55 ($69 monthly) and Suite Professional is $115 ($149 monthly). Enterprise is sales-led and not publicly posted.

Does Zendesk have a free plan?

No. Zendesk offers a 14-day free trial with no card required, but no permanent free plan.

How does Zendesk charge for AI?

AI Agents are included but billed per automated resolution beyond a limited monthly allowance: $1.50 per committed resolution or $2.00 pay-as-you-go. Copilot is a separate $50 per agent per month.
