Kit vs Klaviyo pricing

Kit is built for creators and Klaviyo for ecommerce, but both price on list size. This page compares them using StackScope's email marketing report, last verified October 2026.

Kit's free plan is much larger, covering 10,000 subscribers against 250 active profiles and 500 sends per month for Klaviyo, though Kit's free plan deliberately withholds automation. On paid plans at 1,000 contacts, Klaviyo's Email plan is $30 per month billed monthly with no annual discount, against $33 per month for Kit Creator ($39 billed monthly). At 10,000 contacts Kit Creator is about $117 per month, a rounded annual-effective figure, against $150 for Klaviyo. Klaviyo bills on active profiles, so every contactable subscriber counts, and StackScope notes that Kit's published pricing has moved up since the last check.

FAQ

Common questions

Is Kit cheaper than Klaviyo?

At 10,000 contacts, yes: about $117 per month versus $150. At 1,000 contacts Klaviyo is $30 billed monthly versus $33 for Kit on annual billing ($39 monthly).

Which has the bigger free plan?

Kit's: 10,000 subscribers, without automation beyond 1 basic automation and 1 sequence. Klaviyo's covers 250 active profiles and 500 sends per month.

What is Klaviyo's billing catch?

It bills on active profiles, so every contactable subscriber counts, not just people you actually email.
