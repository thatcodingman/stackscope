Gusto pricing 2026

This page covers Gusto's published prices, free plan limits and the catches, using StackScope's payroll and hr software report, last verified October 2026.

Gusto has no ongoing free plan; the free period ends when the first payroll is run. Simple is $49 per month plus $6 per person, Plus is $80 plus $12 per person and Premium is $180 plus $22 per person, with no separate annual-billing list price published. For 5 employees Simple works out to $79 per month, and for 20 employees $169 per month. Simple supports a single state only, so multi-state payroll requires Plus or Premium; Premium adds dedicated support and advanced HR. The main catch is that support responsiveness and scaling costs are recurring concerns in G2 and Capterra reviews, while praise centers on ease of use and setup.

FAQ

Common questions

How much does Gusto cost?

Simple is $49 per month plus $6 per person, Plus is $80 plus $12 per person and Premium is $180 plus $22 per person. For 5 employees on Simple that is $79 per month, and for 20 employees $169.

Does Gusto have a free plan?

No ongoing free plan. The free period ends when the first payroll is run.

Can Gusto handle multi-state payroll?

Not on Simple, which is single-state only. Multi-state payroll requires Plus or Premium.
