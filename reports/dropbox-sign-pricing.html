Dropbox Sign pricing 2026

This page covers Dropbox Sign's published prices, free plan limits and the catches, using StackScope's e-signature software report, last verified October 2026.

Dropbox Sign's free plan is ongoing and allows 3 signature requests per month (self-signing unlimited) and up to 3 signers per request, without templates or team features. Essentials is a 1-user plan at $10.05 per month annual-equivalent ($120.60 billed annually, $15 billed monthly) with unlimited signature requests and 5 templates. The first team-capable tier is Standard, with a 2-user minimum at $17.50 per user per month on annual billing, about $350/month at 20 users, and 15 templates; Premium has unlimited templates. A current promotion offers 15% off the first 3 months on monthly billing or 30% off the first year on annual billing. The main catch is that teams, branding, in-person signing and bulk sending all require Standard or Premium.

FAQ

Common questions

How much does Dropbox Sign cost?

Essentials is $10.05/month annual-equivalent ($120.60 billed annually, $15 billed monthly) for 1 user. Standard, the first team tier, is $17.50 per user per month on annual billing with a 2-user minimum.

Does Dropbox Sign have a free plan?

Yes. It is ongoing, with 3 signature requests per month (self-signing unlimited) and up to 3 signers per request, but no templates or team features.

Which features need Dropbox Sign Standard or Premium?

Teams, branding, in-person signing and bulk sending.
