Pipedrive vs Zoho CRM pricing

Pipedrive and Zoho CRM have identical annual entry prices, so the difference is in the free plan and monthly billing. This page uses StackScope's CRM report, last verified October 2026.

On annual billing the two are the same: $14, $70 and $280 per month for 1, 5 and 20 users. Billed monthly, Zoho CRM Standard is cheaper at $20, $100 and $400, against $24, $120 and $480 for Pipedrive Lite. Zoho also has an ongoing free plan for 3 users and 5,000 total records, including 5 workflow automation rules, while Pipedrive offers only a 14-day trial. Pipedrive's Lite tier has no automations or sequences, which Growth adds. Pipedrive is praised for ease of use and a clean visual pipeline, while Zoho's catch is a learning curve from its broad feature set.

FAQ

Common questions

Is Pipedrive or Zoho CRM cheaper?

On annual billing they match at $14, $70 and $280 per month for 1, 5 and 20 users. Billed monthly, Zoho is cheaper at $20, $100 and $400 versus $24, $120 and $480 for Pipedrive.

Which has a free plan?

Zoho CRM: 3 users and 5,000 total records, with 5 workflow automation rules. Pipedrive has only a 14-day trial.

What are the main catches?

Pipedrive Lite has no automations or sequences. Zoho's broad feature set creates a learning curve as customization deepens.
