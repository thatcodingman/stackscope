Sketch vs Canva pricing

Sketch is a Mac-native interface design tool and Canva is a broad graphic design platform. This page compares their prices using StackScope's design tools report, last verified October 2026.

For one person the annual prices match at $12 per month, though billed monthly Sketch Standard is $14 against $18 for Canva Pro. For teams Sketch is far cheaper: 5 editors cost $60 per month on annual billing by StackScope's arithmetic, against $1,250 per year ($104.17 per month annual-equivalent) for Canva Business, and 20 editors cost $240 per month against $5,000 per year ($416.67 per month). Canva Pro is one-person only, and Canva Teams is no longer available for new sign-ups. Canva's free plan is a one-person plan with 5GB of storage, while Sketch has only a 30-day trial. Sketch's full editing requires the Mac app.

FAQ

Common questions

Is Sketch cheaper than Canva?

For teams, yes: $60 per month for 5 editors versus $1,250 per year for Canva Business, and $240 per month for 20 versus $5,000 per year. For one person the annual price is $12 on both.

Can teams use Canva Pro?

No. Pro is one-person only, and the team benchmark is Canva Business at $250 per person per year.

Do Sketch and Canva have free plans?

Canva's is an ongoing one-person plan with 5GB of storage. Sketch offers a 30-day trial only, though Viewers are free and unlimited.
