Klaviyo vs ActiveCampaign pricing

Klaviyo is built around ecommerce data and ActiveCampaign around automation depth. This page compares their prices using StackScope's email marketing report, last verified October 2026.

The two price on different units, so compare carefully. Klaviyo's Email plan bills monthly on active profiles, at $20 for 500, $30 for 1,000 and $150 for 10,000, with no annual-prepay discount on self-serve plans. ActiveCampaign Starter is $39 per month at 2,500 contacts, Plus is $95 at the same count and Pro is $375 at 10,000, all as annual-equivalent rates. At 10,000 contacts Klaviyo's $150 is well below ActiveCampaign Pro's $375, but the plans are not like-for-like. Klaviyo has an ongoing free plan for 250 active profiles and 500 sends per month, while ActiveCampaign has only a 14-day trial capped at 100 contacts. ActiveCampaign add-ons can add 30% or more to the real bill, and Klaviyo counts every contactable subscriber as an active profile.
