PandaDoc pricing 2026

This page covers PandaDoc's published prices, free plan limits and the catches, using StackScope's e-signature software report, last verified October 2026.

PandaDoc's free plan is ongoing with unlimited seats, but it is capped at 60 documents per year and 2 recipients per document, and sending blocks until the next annual cycle unless you upgrade; it lacks the drag-and-drop editor and unlimited eSignatures. Starter is $19 per month on annual billing ($35 billed monthly) for 1 user, $95/month for 5 users ($175 monthly) and $380/month for 20 users ($700 monthly), with unlimited documents and eSignatures and no monthly send cap. The main catch is that advanced workflow capability can become a paid extension: External Automations is a $39 per month per account add-on on Business, included on Enterprise and not available at all on Starter. PandaDoc is praised for ease of use, templates and fast signature workflows.
