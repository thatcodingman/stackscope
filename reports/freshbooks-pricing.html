FreshBooks pricing 2026

This page covers FreshBooks's published prices, free plan limits and the catches, using StackScope's accounting software report, last verified October 2026.

FreshBooks has a 30-day free trial but no ongoing free version. Lite is $23 per month, Plus is $43 per month with 50 billable clients plus receipt scanning and accounting reports ($464.40 per year billed annually), and Premium is $70 per month with unlimited clients ($756 per year billed annually); a Select tier above this is Contact Sales. FreshBooks' pricing page still shows 90% off for 3 months, while its own formal offer terms previously stated that promotion ended August 18, 2026, and StackScope has not resolved that conflict. The main catch is that client caps and paid team-member add-ons can raise the real cost as a business grows. Capterra currently lists Lite at $21, Plus at $38 and Premium at $65. Praise centers on ease of use, invoicing and quick setup.

FAQ

Common questions

How much does FreshBooks cost?

Lite is $23 per month, Plus is $43 per month (50 billable clients) and Premium is $70 per month (unlimited clients). Select, above Premium, is Contact Sales.

Does FreshBooks have a free plan?

No. There is a 30-day free trial but no ongoing free version.

Why does FreshBooks cost more as you grow?

Client caps and paid team-member add-ons can raise the real cost. Plus is limited to 50 billable clients, and Premium is unlimited.
