Rippling vs ADP pricing

Rippling and ADP both hide their payroll prices behind quotes. This page lays out what each publishes, using StackScope's payroll and HR report, last verified October 2026.

Neither publishes a self-serve payroll price, so StackScope does not estimate one. Rippling's live pricing page routes to a custom quote, though its own 2026 content gives a reference figure of $35 per month base plus $8 per employee per month, which is not shown as a checkout price. Rippling is modular, with additional modules priced separately. ADP's Essential Payroll, Enhanced Payroll and HR Pro tiers are quote-only, and ADP currently advertises 3 months free on its RUN page as a promotion. Neither has a permanent free plan. G2 says Rippling has no free trial while Capterra says it does, a conflict StackScope has not resolved.

FAQ

Common questions

Is Rippling or ADP cheaper?

Neither publishes a self-serve payroll price, so StackScope can't say. Rippling's own content cites a reference of $35 per month plus $8 per employee, not a list price. ADP requires a quote for every tier.

Do Rippling and ADP have free plans?

Neither has a permanent free plan. ADP advertises 3 months free as a promotion. Sources disagree on whether Rippling offers a free trial.

How does Rippling price add-ons?

Modules such as benefits, time tracking and IT are priced separately, and the full rate card is not public.
