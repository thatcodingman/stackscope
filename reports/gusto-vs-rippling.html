Gusto vs Rippling pricing

Gusto and Rippling are both popular with growing small businesses, but only one publishes self-serve payroll prices. This page shows what is publicly posted for each, using StackScope's payroll and HR report, last verified October 2026.

Gusto publishes its prices: Simple is $49 per month plus $6 per person, Plus is $80 plus $12 per person, and Premium is $180 plus $22 per person. For 5 employees Simple works out to $79 per month, and for 20 employees $169. Gusto's Simple tier is single-state only. Rippling's live pricing page does not publish a self-serve payroll price and routes to a custom quote, and its modules (benefits, time tracking, IT) are priced separately. Rippling's own 2026 content cites reference figures of $35 per month plus $8 per employee, but those are not presented as a posted price, so no direct price comparison can be made. Neither has an ongoing free plan.

FAQ

Common questions

Is Gusto or Rippling cheaper?

That can't be confirmed from public data. Gusto publishes its prices (Simple starts at $49 per month plus $6 per person), while Rippling's pricing page requires a custom quote for payroll. Rippling's own 2026 content cites $35 per month plus $8 per employee as reference figures, but not as a posted price.

Does Gusto or Rippling have a free plan?

Neither has an ongoing free plan. Gusto's free period ends when the first payroll is run, and Rippling offers a trial or promotion only. Sources disagree on whether Rippling offers a free trial, so confirm with the vendor.

Does Gusto support multi-state payroll?

Not on Simple, which is single-state only. Multi-state payroll requires Plus or Premium.
