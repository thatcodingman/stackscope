Asana pricing 2026

Asana has a free plan and two published paid tiers, with Enterprise on quote. This page covers each tier, how AI is capped, and what the free plan allows, using StackScope's project management report, last verified October 2026.

Starter is $10.99 per user ($13.49 billed monthly) with unlimited automations included, and Advanced is $24.99 per user ($30.49 monthly). Enterprise and Enterprise+ have no published price. The free plan is limited to 2 users and includes unlimited tasks and projects, with advanced reporting, workload and approvals gated to paid tiers. The main catch is that AI usage and add-ons push the real bill up: AI is capped even on paid tiers, at 5 AI requests per user per month (50 per account at most) on both Starter and Advanced. Asana is consistently praised as easy to pick up.

FAQ

Common questions

How much does Asana cost per user?

Starter is $10.99 per user ($13.49 billed monthly) and Advanced is $24.99 per user ($30.49 monthly). Enterprise and Enterprise+ have no published price.

Is Asana free?

Yes, up to 2 users, with unlimited tasks and projects. Advanced reporting, workload and approvals require a paid tier.

Does Asana limit AI use?

Yes, even on paid tiers: 5 AI requests per user per month, with a 50-request per-account maximum, on both Starter and Advanced.
