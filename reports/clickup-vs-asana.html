ClickUp vs Asana pricing

ClickUp and Asana both offer a free plan and compete on price per member. This page compares their free-plan limits, paid tiers and AI terms, using StackScope's project management report, last verified October 2026.

ClickUp is cheaper at both published paid tiers: Unlimited is $7 per member ($10 billed monthly) against $10.99 ($13.49 monthly) for Asana Starter, and Business is $12 ($19 monthly) against $24.99 ($30.49 monthly) for Asana Advanced. The free plans trade off differently: ClickUp's has unlimited members but only 60MB of total shared storage, while Asana's is limited to 2 users with unlimited tasks and projects. On AI, ClickUp's Brain add-on is $9 per member per month, while Asana caps AI at 5 requests per user per month on Starter and Advanced. ClickUp gates Goals, time tracking and workload management behind Business despite the "Unlimited" plan name.

FAQ

Common questions

Is ClickUp cheaper than Asana?

At both published paid tiers, yes. ClickUp Unlimited is $7 per member ($10 billed monthly) versus $10.99 ($13.49 monthly) for Asana Starter, and ClickUp Business is $12 ($19 monthly) versus $24.99 ($30.49 monthly) for Asana Advanced.

Which has the better free plan, ClickUp or Asana?

They trade off. ClickUp has unlimited members but only 60MB of total shared storage across the team. Asana is limited to 2 users but has unlimited tasks and projects.

How do ClickUp and Asana price AI?

ClickUp's Brain AI add-on is $9 per member per month, per its own 2026 changelog. Asana caps AI at 5 requests per user per month, with a 50-request per-account maximum, on both Starter and Advanced.
