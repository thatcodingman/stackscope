Salesforce pricing 2026

Salesforce's entry product is Starter Suite, with a free tier below it. This page covers what each costs, how its AI is priced, and where the complexity shows up, using StackScope's CRM report, last verified October 2026.

Starter Suite is $25/month for 1 user, $125/month for 5 users and $500/month for 20 users, the same whether billed annually or monthly, per Salesforce's own pricing page. The Free Suite supports 2 users as an ongoing plan, but access requires logging in at least once every 60 days, and free users get Agentforce Help instead of Standard Support. Its contact or record cap isn't consistently documented in current public materials, so StackScope treats it as unconfirmed. Starter includes assistive AI (Employee Agent) at no extra charge, while Agentforce consumption is purchased separately: $500 per 100,000 Flex Credits, $2 per conversation, or a $5/user/month Agentforce User License. The main catch is complexity and a learning curve, with costs rising as you add customization and premium capabilities.
