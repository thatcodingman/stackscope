FAQ

Common questions

Does HubSpot have a free CRM plan?

Yes — HubSpot's free plan supports 2 users and 1,000 contacts, plus up to 1,000,000 records on other standard objects, and it's an ongoing plan, not a trial. It's missing advanced automation and workflows, which require the paid Sales Hub Starter tier ($20/month) or higher.

Which CRM is cheapest for a 5-person team?

Pipedrive and Zoho CRM tie for cheapest at 5 users — both $70/month billed annually ($120/month for Pipedrive, $100/month for Zoho if billed monthly). HubSpot and Salesforce cost more at that size ($100/month and $125/month respectively), though they include more built-in automation out of the box.

Does Salesforce have a free plan?

Yes — Salesforce's Free Suite supports 2 users as an ongoing plan, not a trial, though it requires logging in at least once every 60 days to stay active. Its exact contact/record cap isn't consistently documented in Salesforce's current public materials.

What AI features are included in these CRMs without extra cost?

HubSpot's Starter plan includes 500 AI credits (additional credits cost $0.010 each). Salesforce's Starter Suite includes assistive AI (Employee Agent) at no charge, with paid Agentforce usage separate. Pipedrive's Lite tier now ships with AI-generated reports and a Sales Assistant included. Zoho's Zia Agents require the Professional tier or higher, but include up to 30 million hosted-LLM tokens per month.
