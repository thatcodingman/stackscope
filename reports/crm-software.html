Figures are aggregated from vendor pricing pages and independent third-party pricing research as of August 2026, and reflect published list prices at three fixed seat counts (1 / 5 / 20 users) on each vendor's cheapest paid tier — actual cost depends on add-ons and negotiated terms. HubSpot's $7/$35/$140 figures are a limited-time promotional rate for new customers; the standard rate shown in the main columns ($20/$100/$400) applies regardless of billing frequency. Salesforce's free-plan contact/record cap could not be confirmed to one consistent number — flagged rather than guessed. Confirm current pricing directly with each vendor before purchasing. This is not a recommendation to buy any specific product.

Cheapest at each size: Pipedrive and Zoho tie for cheapest at all three breakpoints ($14/mo at 1 user, $70/mo at 5, $280/mo at 20, annual billing) — but Pipedrive's Lite tier has no automation or email sync at any seat count, while Zoho's Standard tier includes more out of the box. HubSpot's standard rate and Salesforce sit meaningfully higher across the board; HubSpot's promotional new-customer rate would undercut both, but only temporarily. If price is what matters most, Zoho's cheapest tier does more for the money than Pipedrive's; if automation depth matters more than price, Salesforce and HubSpot's more expensive entry points reflect real capability gaps, not just markup.
