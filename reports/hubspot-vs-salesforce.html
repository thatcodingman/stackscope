HubSpot vs Salesforce pricing

HubSpot and Salesforce are the two best-known CRMs, and their entry tiers are priced very close together. This page puts their cheapest paid tiers, free plans and AI terms side by side, using the figures from StackScope's CRM report, last verified October 2026.

HubSpot's Sales Hub Starter is cheaper than Salesforce's Starter Suite at every seat count measured: $20 vs. $25 for 1 user, $100 vs. $125 for 5 users, and $400 vs. $500 for 20 users. Both have a genuinely ongoing free plan for 2 users, but they differ on AI: Salesforce bundles assistive AI (Employee Agent) at no extra charge, while HubSpot's Starter includes 500 AI credits and charges $0.010 per additional credit. HubSpot is also running a limited-time new-customer promotion, but the standard rate is the one to budget around.

FAQ

Common questions

Is HubSpot cheaper than Salesforce?

On the cheapest paid tier of each, yes. HubSpot Sales Hub Starter costs $20/month for 1 user, $100/month for 5 users and $400/month for 20 users. Salesforce Starter Suite costs $25, $125 and $500 at the same seat counts.

Do HubSpot and Salesforce both have free plans?

Yes, both are ongoing free plans rather than trials. HubSpot's free plan supports 2 users and 1,000 contacts. Salesforce's Free Suite supports 2 users, but requires logging in at least once every 60 days, and its exact contact or record cap isn't consistently documented in current public materials.

How do HubSpot and Salesforce price AI features?

HubSpot's Starter plan includes 500 AI credits, with additional credits at $0.010 each. Salesforce's Starter Suite includes assistive AI (Employee Agent) at no extra charge, with Agentforce usage purchased separately.
