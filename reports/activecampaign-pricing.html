ActiveCampaign pricing 2026

This page covers ActiveCampaign's published prices, free plan limits and the catches, using StackScope's email marketing software report, last verified October 2026.

ActiveCampaign has no free plan, only a 14-day trial capped at 100 contacts. Starter is $39/month at 2,500 contacts, Plus is $95/month at 2,500 contacts and Pro is $375/month at 10,000 contacts, all as annual-equivalent rates from the vendor's contact-based calculator; a consistent public month-to-month figure isn't posted at every contact level. The main catch is that public pricing is now built around annual billing and a contact-count selector rather than a flat monthly rate. Starter's automation actions are capped at 5 per automation, which pushes serious users to Plus. Automation depth is consistently the most praised feature, including branching, multi-step workflows that most competitors reserve for pricier tiers.
