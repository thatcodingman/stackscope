Kit pricing 2026

This page covers Kit's published prices, free plan limits and the catches, using StackScope's email marketing software report, last verified October 2026.

Kit's free plan covers 10,000 subscribers with unlimited broadcasts, forms, landing pages and digital products, plus 1 basic automation and 1 email sequence, and is ongoing rather than a trial. Creator is $33/month at 1,000 subscribers ($39 billed monthly), $49/month at 3,000 subscribers ($59 monthly) and about $117/month at 10,000 subscribers ($139 monthly); the 10,000 figure is an annual-effective number rounded from Kit's schedule, so confirm the exact total in Kit's own calculator. The main catch is that the free plan is huge on subscribers but deliberately withholds automation. Kit is praised for simplicity and creator-focused tools, including built-in digital product sales and a cross-promotion network.

FAQ

Common questions

How much does Kit cost?

Creator is $33/month at 1,000 subscribers ($39 billed monthly), $49/month at 3,000 ($59 monthly) and about $117/month at 10,000 ($139 monthly). Confirm the 10,000-subscriber total in Kit's own calculator.

Does Kit have a free plan?

Yes: up to 10,000 subscribers with unlimited broadcasts, forms, landing pages and digital products, plus 1 basic automation and 1 email sequence. It is ongoing, not a trial.

What is the catch with Kit's free plan?

It is generous on subscribers but deliberately withholds automation.
