Pipedrive pricing 2026

This page covers Pipedrive's published prices, free plan limits and the catches, using StackScope's crm software report, last verified October 2026.

Pipedrive has no free plan, only a 14-day trial. Lite is $14 per user per month on annual billing ($24 billed monthly), which is $70/month for 5 users ($120 monthly) and $280/month for 20 users ($480 monthly). The main catch is that automation and two-way email sync are deliberately withheld from Lite: there are no automations or sequences at all, and Growth adds both. Lite now ships with AI-generated reports and a Sales Assistant, and no separate mandatory AI-credit price was found on the current vendor pricing material. Pipedrive is praised for ease of use and a clean visual pipeline. Capterra currently shows a conflicting $19 figure for Lite, while the vendor page and G2 both show $14 annual and $24 monthly.

FAQ

Common questions

How much does Pipedrive cost?

Lite is $14 per user per month on annual billing ($24 billed monthly): $70/month for 5 users ($120 monthly) and $280/month for 20 users ($480 monthly).

Does Pipedrive have a free plan?

No. Pipedrive offers a 14-day trial only, with no permanent free edition.

What is missing from Pipedrive's Lite tier?

Automations, sequences and two-way email sync. Growth adds both.
