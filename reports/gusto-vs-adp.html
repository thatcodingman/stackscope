Gusto vs ADP pricing

Gusto publishes its payroll prices and ADP does not, which is the main difference for small teams. This page compares them using StackScope's payroll and HR report, last verified October 2026.

Gusto's Simple plan is $49 per month plus $6 per person, which is $79 per month for 5 employees, $169 for 20 and $349 for 50. Plus is $80 per month plus $12 per person and Premium is $180 plus $22 per person. ADP's tiers, including Essential Payroll, Enhanced Payroll and HR Pro, have no public list price: pricing for 5, 20 or 50 employees requires a quote, and StackScope does not estimate it. ADP currently advertises 3 months free on its RUN page, which is a promotion rather than a permanent free tier. Neither has an ongoing free plan, and Gusto's free period ends when you run your first payroll. Gusto's Simple tier is single-state only, and multi-state payroll requires Plus or Premium.

FAQ

Common questions

Is Gusto cheaper than ADP?

Gusto's prices are public, at $79 per month for 5 employees on Simple, while ADP requires a quote for every tier, so StackScope cannot say which is cheaper for your business without one.

Does ADP publish its prices?

No. Essential Payroll, Enhanced Payroll and HR Pro are quote-only, and the current 3-months-free promotion is separate from the standard price.

Does Gusto cover multi-state payroll?

Not on Simple, which is single-state only. Multi-state payroll requires Plus or Premium.
