QuickBooks Online pricing 2026

QuickBooks Online prices by tier and user limit, and has no ongoing free plan. This page covers all four published tiers and the current new-customer discount, using StackScope's accounting report, last verified October 2026.

Simple Start is $38/month and allows 1 user plus 2 accountant accesses. Essentials is $85/month with 3 users plus 2 accountants, Plus is $140/month with 5 users plus 2 accountants, and Advanced is $340/month with 25 users plus 3 accountants. Intuit currently advertises 50% off for 3 months for new customers, which puts Simple Start at $19/month during the promo; free trials are not eligible. There is a 30-day free trial but no ongoing free plan. The main catch, per G2 reviews, is that price increases and support inconsistency recur. QuickBooks is praised for ease of use, broad accounting workflows, bank connections and integrations.
