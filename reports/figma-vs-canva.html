Figma vs Canva pricing

Figma and Canva overlap on design work but are priced for different teams. This page compares seat pricing, free-plan limits and team options using StackScope's design tools report, last verified October 2026.

Figma Professional is $16 per editor per month on annual billing ($20 monthly), and scales to $80 for 5 editors and $320 for 20. Canva Pro is cheaper at $12 per month ($144 per year), but it is a one-person plan regardless of what you pay, so a team needs Canva Business at $250 per person per year ($20.83 per month annual-equivalent, $25 monthly). That makes Canva Business $1,250 a year for 5 people and $5,000 a year for 20. Both have an ongoing free plan; Figma's includes 3 team files and unlimited viewers, while Canva's is a one-person plan with 5GB of storage. Canva Teams is no longer available for new sign-ups.

FAQ

Common questions

Is Figma or Canva cheaper?

For one person Canva Pro is cheaper at $12 per month on annual billing versus $16 for a Figma Professional editor seat. For teams Canva requires Business at $250 per person per year ($20.83 per month annual-equivalent), which is more than Figma's $16 per editor per month.

Does Canva Pro work for teams?

No. Canva Pro is a one-person plan regardless of price. A team needs Canva Business, and Canva Teams is no longer available for new sign-ups or upgrades.

What does Figma's free plan include?

An ongoing free plan with 3 team files, unlimited personal drafts, 30-day version history, 500 AI credits per month and unlimited free viewers. Unlimited team files and advanced collaboration and admin features require a paid plan.
