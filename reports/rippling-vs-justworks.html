Rippling vs Justworks pricing

Justworks publishes its payroll prices and Rippling does not, which is the main difference for small teams. This page compares them using StackScope's payroll and HR report, last verified October 2026.

Justworks Payroll is $8 per employee per month plus a $50 base, which is $90 per month for 5 employees, $210 for 20 and $450 for 50. Rippling's live pricing page does not publish a self-serve payroll price, so StackScope does not estimate one, though Rippling's own 2026 content cites a reference of $35 per month base plus $8 per employee per month, not a checkout price. Justworks also sells PEO Basic at $79 per employee per month, a separate co-employment model rather than a pricier payroll tier, and advertises 3 months free for Payroll as a promotion. Rippling has no permanent free plan, and its add-on modules are priced separately.

Is Justworks cheaper than Rippling?

Justworks Payroll is $90 per month for 5 employees and $210 for 20. Rippling publishes no self-serve price, so StackScope can't compare directly.

What is Justworks PEO?

A separate co-employment model, not a pricier payroll tier. PEO Basic is $79 per employee per month.

Do either have free plans?

Neither has a permanent free plan. Justworks advertises 3 months free for Payroll as a promotion.
