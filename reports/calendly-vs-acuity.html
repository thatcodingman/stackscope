Calendly vs Acuity Scheduling pricing

Calendly and Acuity Scheduling price scheduling in different ways: Calendly per seat, Acuity per account with a calendar allowance. This page shows how that changes the cost for 1, 5 and 20 people, using StackScope's scheduling report, last verified October 2026.

For a single person Calendly Standard ($10 per month on annual billing) is cheaper than Acuity Starter ($16 per month for 1 calendar). As a team grows the order reverses, because Acuity is priced per account: its Standard tier covers 6 calendars for $27 per month and Premium covers 36 calendars for $49 per month, against Calendly's $50 for 5 seats and $200 for 20. Calendly also has an ongoing free plan (1 user, 1 event type, 1 connected calendar), while Acuity offers only a 7-day trial. Acuity Premium includes its AI Booking Assistant at no extra charge.

FAQ

Common questions

Is Calendly or Acuity cheaper for a team?

Acuity, at 5 and 20 users. Calendly Standard costs $50 per month for 5 seats and $200 per month for 20 on annual billing. Acuity is priced per account: $27 per month (Standard, 6 calendars) and $49 per month (Premium, 36 calendars). For 1 user Calendly is cheaper at $10 versus $16.

Does Calendly or Acuity have a free plan?

Calendly has an ongoing free plan for 1 user with 1 event type and 1 connected calendar. Acuity has no ongoing free plan, only a 7-day trial.

How is Acuity's pricing structured?

Per account rather than per seat, with a calendar limit per tier: Starter has 1 calendar, Standard has 6 and Premium has 36. Hitting the limit means moving to a higher plan, with no overage fee identified.
