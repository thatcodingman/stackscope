Vendor pricing

SavvyCal pricing 2026

This page covers SavvyCal's published prices, free plan limits and the catches, using StackScope's scheduling software report, last verified October 2026.

SavvyCal has no ongoing free tier; the current vendor page offers a free try and a 30-day money-back guarantee. Basic is $10 per seat per month on annual billing ($12 billed monthly, per Capterra), which is $50/month for 5 seats and $200/month for 20 seats annually ($60 and $240 monthly). The main catch is that pricing can feel high for solo users with no permanent free tier, and G2 specifically identifies pricing as the main negative theme in its small review sample. Reviewers praise the calendar-overlay experience, the user experience and flexible availability controls.
