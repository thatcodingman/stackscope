Mailchimp vs Klaviyo pricing

Mailchimp and Klaviyo both price by contact count, which makes a straight comparison tricky. This page lines up their free plans and published tiers, using StackScope's email marketing report, last verified October 2026.

At the 500-contact entry tier Mailchimp Standard is $17/month (annual or $20 monthly) against $20/month for Klaviyo Email. At 10,000 contacts Mailchimp lists $115/month against $150/month for Klaviyo, though Mailchimp's vendor presents that figure as a first-year promotional rate for eligible businesses, not a guaranteed renewal rate. Klaviyo's middle tier is 1,000 profiles while Mailchimp's is 2,500 contacts, so those two rows are not like-for-like. Klaviyo bills on active profiles, so every contactable subscriber counts, and its self-serve plans have no annual-prepay discount. Both offer a 250-contact free plan with 500 sends a month.

FAQ

Common questions

Is Mailchimp cheaper than Klaviyo?

At the entry tier (500 contacts) Mailchimp Standard is $17 per month versus $20 for Klaviyo. At 10,000 contacts Mailchimp lists $115 versus $150 for Klaviyo, but Mailchimp's figure is presented as a first-year promotional rate for eligible businesses. The middle tiers cover different contact counts (2,500 for Mailchimp, 1,000 for Klaviyo), so they can't be compared directly.

Do Mailchimp and Klaviyo have free plans?

Yes. Both free plans cover 250 contacts (Klaviyo counts active profiles) and 500 sends per month. Mailchimp's free plan also caps sending at 250 per day, and pauses sending rather than charging if exceeded.

How does Klaviyo bill?

Klaviyo bills on active profiles, not just people you actually email, so every contactable subscriber counts. Its self-serve plans are billed monthly with no annual-prepay discount.
