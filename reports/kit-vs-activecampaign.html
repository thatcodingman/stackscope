Kit vs ActiveCampaign pricing

Kit is aimed at creators and ActiveCampaign at automation-heavy marketers. This page compares their prices and free plans using StackScope's email marketing report, last verified October 2026.

Kit is cheaper at 10,000 contacts: Creator is about $117 per month, a rounded annual-effective figure, against $375 per month for ActiveCampaign Pro, also annual-equivalent. At smaller sizes the contact counts differ, with Kit Creator at $49 for 3,000 subscribers and ActiveCampaign Starter at $39 for 2,500 contacts, so they are not like-for-like. Kit has an ongoing free plan for 10,000 subscribers, while ActiveCampaign has only a 14-day trial capped at 100 contacts. The trade-off is automation: Kit's free plan has only 1 basic automation and 1 sequence, while ActiveCampaign's depth is its most praised feature, though Starter caps actions at 5 per automation and add-ons can add 30% or more.
