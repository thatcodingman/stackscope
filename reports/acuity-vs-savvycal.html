Acuity Scheduling vs SavvyCal pricing

Acuity prices by calendar count and SavvyCal by seat, so the cheaper one depends on team size. This page compares them using StackScope's scheduling report, last verified October 2026.

For teams Acuity is cheaper: Standard, which includes 6 calendars, is $27 per month on annual billing ($34 monthly), against $50 per month for 5 SavvyCal seats. At 20 calendars Acuity Premium, which includes 36 calendars, is $49 per month ($61 monthly), against $200 for 20 SavvyCal seats. For a single calendar Acuity Starter is $16 per month ($20 monthly), against $10 ($12 monthly) for one SavvyCal seat. Neither has an ongoing free plan: Acuity offers a 7-day trial that blocks confirmation and reminder messages to clients, and SavvyCal offers a free try and a 30-day money-back guarantee. Acuity is account-based, not per-seat, with a hard cap per tier.

FAQ

Common questions

Is Acuity cheaper than SavvyCal?

For teams, yes: $27 per month for 6 calendars versus $50 for 5 SavvyCal seats, and $49 for 36 calendars versus $200 for 20 seats. For one calendar, SavvyCal is $10 versus $16.

Do Acuity and SavvyCal have free plans?

Neither. Acuity has a 7-day trial that blocks client confirmation and reminder messages. SavvyCal has a free try and a 30-day money-back guarantee.

How does Acuity price by calendar?

Starter includes 1 calendar, Standard includes 6 and Premium includes 36. At the limit you move up a tier, with no overage fee identified.
