SignNow pricing 2026

This page covers SignNow's published prices, free plan limits and the catches, using StackScope's e-signature software report, last verified October 2026.

SignNow has no free sending tier, only a 7-day free trial. Business is $8 per month on annual billing ($20 billed monthly) for 1 user, $40/month for 5 users ($100 monthly) and $160/month for 20 users ($400 monthly), and G2 matches the annual figures. The main catch is an unresolved source conflict: SignNow's own pricing and Help pages say 100 envelopes per user per year, and its March 2026 refund policy references overage charges past that limit, but other current SignNow resource pages advertise "no envelope cap." StackScope flags this rather than resolving it, so confirm with SignNow directly. SignNow is praised for ease of use, intuitive signing and templates.

How much does SignNow cost?

Business is $8/month for 1 user on annual billing ($20 billed monthly), $40/month for 5 users ($100 monthly) and $160/month for 20 users ($400 monthly).

Does SignNow have a free plan?

No. There is a 7-day free trial only, with no permanent free sending tier.

Does SignNow cap envelopes?

Sources conflict. SignNow's own pricing and Help pages say 100 envelopes per user per year, but other current resource pages advertise no envelope cap. StackScope has not resolved this, so confirm with SignNow directly.
