Calendly vs YouCanBookMe pricing

Calendly and YouCanBookMe both offer a free plan and a lower-priced entry tier. This page compares them using StackScope's scheduling report, last verified October 2026.

YouCanBookMe is cheaper for one person: Individual is $8.10 per month on annual billing ($9 monthly) and Professional is $11.70 ($13 monthly), against $10 ($12 monthly) for a Calendly Standard seat. The pricing units differ, though. Individual and Professional are account-priced by number of calendar connections, while Teams switches to per-member pricing at $16.20 per member per month annually, which is $324 per month for 20 members against $200 for 20 Calendly seats. Both have ongoing free plans: Calendly's covers 1 user, 1 event type and 1 connected calendar, and YouCanBookMe's covers 1 calendar connection and 1 booking page. Calendly gates advanced team and routing features above Standard.

FAQ

Common questions

Is YouCanBookMe cheaper than Calendly?

For one person, yes: Individual is $8.10 per month on annual billing versus $10 for a Calendly Standard seat. For a team of 20, YouCanBookMe Teams is $324 per month versus $200 for 20 Calendly seats.

Do Calendly and YouCanBookMe have free plans?

Yes, both ongoing. Calendly's covers 1 user, 1 event type and 1 connected calendar. YouCanBookMe's covers 1 calendar connection and 1 booking page.

Why does YouCanBookMe's pricing change for teams?

Individual and Professional are single account prices based on calendar connections, while Teams is priced per member.
