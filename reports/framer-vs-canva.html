Framer vs Canva pricing

Framer builds websites and Canva makes graphics, but teams still compare their prices. This page uses StackScope's design tools report, last verified October 2026.

For one person the prices are close: Framer Basic is $10 per month on annual billing ($15 monthly) against $12 ($18 monthly) for Canva Pro. For teams, Canva Business is $250 per person per year, which comes to $1,250 per year for 5 people ($104.17 per month annual-equivalent) and $5,000 per year for 20 people ($416.67 per month). Framer's arithmetic is $90 per month for 5 editors and $390 for 20 on annual billing. Canva Pro is one-person only, and Canva Teams is no longer available for new sign-ups. Framer's free plan allows up to 3 editors but has no custom domain or production publishing, while Canva's free plan is a one-person plan with 5GB of storage.

FAQ

Common questions

Is Framer cheaper than Canva?

For one person, slightly: $10 versus $12 per month on annual billing. For 5 people, Framer comes to $90 per month versus $1,250 per year for Canva Business. For 20, $390 per month versus $5,000 per year.

Can teams use Canva Pro?

No. Canva Pro is one-person only. The team benchmark is Canva Business at $250 per person per year, and Canva Teams is no longer available for new sign-ups.

Do Framer and Canva have free plans?

Both ongoing. Framer's allows up to 3 editors but has no custom domain or production publishing. Canva's is a one-person plan with 5GB of storage.
