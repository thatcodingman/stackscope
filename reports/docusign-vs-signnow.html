DocuSign vs SignNow pricing

DocuSign is the best-known e-signature tool and SignNow is a lower-priced rival. This page compares their prices using StackScope's e-signature report, last verified October 2026.

SignNow is cheaper for teams: Business is $8 per month on annual billing for 1 user, $40 for 5 and $160 for 20 ($20, $100 and $400 billed monthly). DocuSign's Personal plan is $10 per month but is 1 user only, and its first team-capable tier, Standard, is $25 per user per month on annual billing, about $500 per month at 20 users. SignNow has only a 7-day trial, while DocuSign lists an ongoing 1-user Personal plan whose send limit is not exposed as one clean vendor figure. SignNow carries an unresolved source conflict: its own pages say 100 envelopes per user per year, while other pages advertise no envelope cap. StackScope flags it rather than resolving it, so confirm with SignNow before relying on either claim.

FAQ

Common questions

Is SignNow cheaper than DocuSign?

For teams, yes: SignNow Business is $40 per month for 5 users and $160 for 20 on annual billing, while DocuSign Standard is $25 per user per month, about $500 at 20 users.

Does SignNow limit envelopes?

Unresolved. SignNow's own pricing pages say 100 envelopes per user per year, but other current pages advertise no envelope cap. Confirm with SignNow directly.

Do DocuSign and SignNow have free plans?

SignNow offers only a 7-day trial. DocuSign lists an ongoing 1-user Personal plan, and its send limit is not exposed as one clean figure.
