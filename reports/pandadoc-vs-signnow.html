PandaDoc vs SignNow pricing

PandaDoc and SignNow both sell per-seat plans for document signing, at very different prices. This page compares them using StackScope's e-signature report, last verified October 2026.

SignNow is cheaper at every size measured. Business is $8 per month on annual billing for 1 user, $40 for 5 and $160 for 20, against $19, $95 and $380 for PandaDoc Starter. Billed monthly the gap is wider still: $20, $100 and $400 for SignNow versus $35, $175 and $700 for PandaDoc. PandaDoc has an ongoing free plan with unlimited seats, 60 documents per year and 2 recipients per document, while SignNow has only a 7-day trial. PandaDoc Starter includes unlimited documents and eSignatures. SignNow has an unresolved source conflict: its own pages say 100 envelopes per user per year, while other pages advertise no envelope cap, so confirm with SignNow before relying on either claim.

FAQ

Common questions

Is SignNow cheaper than PandaDoc?

Yes at every size measured: $8, $40 and $160 per month for 1, 5 and 20 users on annual billing versus $19, $95 and $380 for PandaDoc Starter.

Do PandaDoc and SignNow have free plans?

PandaDoc does: unlimited seats, 60 documents per year and 2 recipients per document. SignNow has only a 7-day trial.

Does SignNow cap envelopes?

Unresolved. SignNow's own pages say 100 envelopes per user per year, while other pages advertise no cap. Confirm with SignNow.
