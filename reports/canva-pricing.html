Canva pricing 2026

This page covers Canva's published prices, free plan limits and the catches, using StackScope's design tools report, last verified October 2026.

Canva's free plan is a one-person plan with 5GB of storage and a limited AI allowance. Pro is $12 per month on annual billing ($144 per year, or $18 billed monthly), includes 100GB of storage, and remains one-person only regardless of price. A team needs Canva Business at $250 per person per year ($20.83 per month annual-equivalent, $25 billed monthly), which is $1,250 per year for 5 people and $5,000 per year for 20. Canva Teams is no longer available for new sign-ups or upgrades. The main catch is that premium features and content are gated, and any real team ends up on Business. Canva is praised for ease of use and a large template library. Some older third-party pages still show a stale $180 per year Pro figure.

FAQ

Common questions

How much does Canva Pro cost?

$12 per month on annual billing ($144 per year), or $18 billed monthly. It is a one-person plan.

What does Canva cost for a team?

Canva Business is $250 per person per year ($20.83 per month annual-equivalent, $25 billed monthly): $1,250 per year for 5 people and $5,000 per year for 20. Canva Teams is no longer available for new sign-ups.

Is Canva free?

Yes. The free plan is a one-person plan with 5GB of storage and a limited AI allowance.
