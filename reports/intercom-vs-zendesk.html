Intercom vs Zendesk pricing

Intercom and Zendesk are priced per seat, and both bill extra for AI. This page compares their published tiers and AI charges using StackScope's help desk report, last verified October 2026.

Zendesk is cheaper at every published tier: its Support Team is $19 per agent ($25 billed monthly), Suite Team is $55 ($69 monthly) and Suite Professional is $115 ($149 monthly), against Intercom's Essential at $29 per seat ($39 monthly), Advanced at $85 ($99 monthly) and Expert at $132 ($139 monthly). The tier names don't map exactly. Both bill AI on top of seats: Intercom's Fin charges $0.99 per outcome and its Copilot adds $29 per agent per month billed annually, while Zendesk's AI Agents charge $1.50 per committed resolution or $2.00 pay-as-you-go beyond a monthly allowance, and its Copilot is $50 per agent per month. Neither has a free plan, only a 14-day trial with no card required.

FAQ

Common questions

Is Zendesk cheaper than Intercom?

At every published tier, yes: Zendesk is $19, $55 and $115 per agent against Intercom's $29, $85 and $132 per seat, on the billing basis shown. The tiers don't map one-to-one, so check what each includes.

How do Intercom and Zendesk charge for AI?

Intercom's Fin bills $0.99 per outcome on top of seats, and its Copilot adds $29 per agent per month billed annually. Zendesk's AI Agents bill $1.50 per committed resolution or $2.00 pay-as-you-go beyond a limited monthly allowance, and its Copilot is $50 per agent per month.

Do Intercom and Zendesk have free plans?

Neither. Both offer a 14-day free trial with no card required.
