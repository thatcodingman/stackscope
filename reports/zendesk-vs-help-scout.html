Zendesk vs Help Scout pricing

Zendesk is the enterprise-grade help desk and Help Scout is the inbox-style alternative for smaller teams. This page compares their prices using StackScope's help desk report, last verified October 2026.

Zendesk has the cheaper entry tier: Support Team is $19 per agent ($25 billed monthly), against $25 per user ($30 monthly) for Help Scout Standard. Higher up, Zendesk Suite Team is $55 ($69 monthly) and Suite Professional is $115 ($149 monthly), against Help Scout Plus at $45 ($54 monthly) and Pro at $75 ($90 monthly, with a 10-seat minimum), and the tiers do not map one-to-one. Help Scout has an ongoing free plan for up to 5 users with 100 contacts per month and 1 inbox, while Zendesk has only a 14-day trial. AI is priced differently: Zendesk's AI Agents charge $1.50 per committed resolution or $2.00 pay-as-you-go beyond a limited allowance, with Copilot at $50 per agent per month, while Help Scout's AI Answers is $0.75 per resolution after a three-month free trial.

FAQ

Common questions

Is Zendesk cheaper than Help Scout?

At the entry tier, yes: Zendesk Support Team is $19 per agent versus $25 per user for Help Scout Standard. Help Scout also has a free plan for up to 5 users, which Zendesk lacks.

Does Help Scout have a free plan?

Yes: an ongoing plan for up to 5 users with 100 contacts per month and 1 inbox. Zendesk offers only a 14-day free trial with no card required.

How do Zendesk and Help Scout charge for AI?

Zendesk's AI Agents cost $1.50 per committed resolution or $2.00 pay-as-you-go beyond a limited allowance, and Copilot is $50 per agent per month. Help Scout's AI Answers costs $0.75 per resolution after a three-month free trial.
