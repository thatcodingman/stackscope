Dropbox Sign vs SignNow pricing

Dropbox Sign and SignNow are both lower-priced alternatives to DocuSign. This page compares them using StackScope's e-signature report, last verified October 2026.

SignNow is cheaper for both individuals and teams. For one user, SignNow Business is $8 per month on annual billing ($20 monthly) against $10.05 ($15 monthly) for Dropbox Sign Essentials, though Dropbox Sign is cheaper billed monthly at that size. For teams, SignNow is $160 per month at 20 users on annual billing, against about $350 for Dropbox Sign Standard at $17.50 per user, which has a 2-user minimum. Dropbox Sign has an ongoing free plan with 3 signature requests per month, while SignNow has only a 7-day trial. SignNow's own pages say 100 envelopes per user per year while others advertise no cap, an unresolved conflict StackScope flags rather than resolves.

FAQ

Common questions

Is SignNow cheaper than Dropbox Sign?

On annual billing, yes: $8 versus $10.05 for one user, and $160 versus about $350 at 20 users. Billed monthly at one user, Dropbox Sign Essentials is cheaper at $15 versus $20.

Which has a free plan?

Dropbox Sign: 3 signature requests per month and up to 3 signers per request. SignNow has only a 7-day trial.

What is Dropbox Sign's team minimum?

Standard, its first team-capable tier, has a 2-user minimum at $17.50 per user per month on annual billing.
