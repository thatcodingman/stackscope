QuickBooks Online vs Xero pricing

QuickBooks Online and Xero are the two leading small-business accounting tools in the US, and Xero raised its prices on October 1, 2026. This page compares both vendors' published tiers using StackScope's accounting report, last verified October 2026.

Xero is cheaper at every matched tier even after its October 1, 2026 increase: Early is $27 per month against $38 for QuickBooks Simple Start, Growing is $59 against $85 for Essentials, and Established is $97 against $340 for QuickBooks Advanced. The tiers are not identical, since QuickBooks limits Simple Start to 1 user plus 2 accountant accesses, so check the user limits before comparing. Neither has an ongoing free plan, only a 30-day free trial, and Xero includes accountant and bookkeeper access at no extra cost. Intuit currently advertises 50% off for 3 months for new QuickBooks customers.

FAQ

Common questions

Is Xero cheaper than QuickBooks?

At the matched entry and mid tiers, yes. Xero Early is $27 per month versus $38 for QuickBooks Simple Start, and Xero Growing is $59 versus $85 for QuickBooks Essentials. This reflects Xero's price increase effective October 1, 2026, from $25, $55 and $90.

Do QuickBooks Online and Xero have free plans?

Neither has an ongoing free plan. Both offer a 30-day free trial.

How much did Xero raise its prices?

Effective October 1, 2026, Xero raised Early from $25 to $27 per month, Growing from $55 to $59 and Established from $90 to $97. The increase applies to new and existing US subscribers.
