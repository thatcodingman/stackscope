Payroll & HR software — full comparison

Payroll pricing varies by platform model: per-employee SaaS, modular HR/payroll, or PEO. Monthly prices are shown where public; annual figures are annualized monthly equivalents unless a separate annual-billing price is published.

Pricing model: Gusto — published base fee + per-employee SaaS pricing. Rippling — modular SaaS; only the core HR platform has a published starting price, and actual Payroll pricing is quote-dependent. ADP — fully sales-quoted payroll, no public list price at any tier. Justworks — Payroll uses published base + per-employee pricing; PEO Basic/Plus are a separate co-employment model, not a pricier tier of the same product.
