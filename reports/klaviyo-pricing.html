Klaviyo pricing 2026

Klaviyo bills on active profiles rather than contacts you email. This page covers its free plan, the published Email plan tiers, and what that billing model means, using StackScope's email marketing report, last verified October 2026.

The free plan covers 250 active profiles and 500 sends per month, is ongoing rather than a trial, and also includes a $5/month mobile-message allowance and a $5/month Composer AI allowance. The Email plan is $20/month at 500 profiles, $30/month at 1,000 profiles and $150/month at 10,000 profiles, billed monthly, with no annual-prepay discount on self-serve plans. The main catch is that Klaviyo bills on active profiles, not just people you actually email, so every contactable subscriber counts. Klaviyo is praised for deep, ecommerce-native segmentation and behavior-based personalization without a steep learning curve.

FAQ

Common questions

How much does Klaviyo cost?

The Email plan is $20/month at 500 profiles, $30/month at 1,000 profiles and $150/month at 10,000 profiles, billed monthly. There is no annual-prepay discount on self-serve plans.

Does Klaviyo have a free plan?

Yes: 250 active profiles and 500 sends per month, ongoing rather than a trial, plus a $5/month mobile-message allowance and a $5/month Composer AI allowance.

What are active profiles in Klaviyo?

Klaviyo bills on active profiles, not just people you actually email, so every contactable subscriber counts toward your tier.
