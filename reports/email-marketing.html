FAQ

Common questions

Which email marketing tool has the most generous free plan?

Kit, with 10,000 free subscribers — far more than Mailchimp (250 contacts) or Klaviyo (250 active profiles). The tradeoff is automation: Kit's free plan only includes 1 basic automation and 1 email sequence. ActiveCampaign has no free plan at all, only a 14-day trial capped at 100 contacts.

Does Mailchimp charge for unsubscribed contacts?

Yes — this is a common billing trap. Mailchimp bills for every contact in your audience, including unsubscribed ones, unless you manually archive them. Monthly send allowances are also capped at a multiple of contact count (10x on Essentials, 12x on Standard, 15x on Premium), and exceeding that can trigger overage charges.

Is ActiveCampaign's pricing the same every month?

Not exactly — ActiveCampaign's public pricing page is built around annual billing and a contact-count selector rather than a flat, consistently published month-to-month rate. The figures most comparisons cite (including $39/$95/$375 at 2,500/2,500/10,000 contacts) are annual-equivalent rates from the vendor's own calculator. G2 currently lists lower starting figures ($15/$49/$79/$145), which reflects a different presentation, not a confirmed lower price.

How does Klaviyo's billing work if I have inactive subscribers?

Klaviyo bills on "active profiles" — every contactable subscriber counts toward your plan's profile limit, not just the people you actually email. This is a key difference from send-volume-based pricing and can push you into a higher tier faster than expected if your list grows but engagement doesn't.
