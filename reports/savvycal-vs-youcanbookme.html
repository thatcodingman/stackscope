SavvyCal vs YouCanBookMe pricing

SavvyCal and YouCanBookMe are both modern scheduling tools with different pricing units. This page compares them using StackScope's scheduling report, last verified October 2026.

YouCanBookMe is cheaper for one person: Individual is $8.10 per month on annual billing ($9 monthly) and Professional is $11.70 ($13 monthly), against $10 ($12 monthly) for a SavvyCal seat. For a 20-person team SavvyCal is cheaper at $200 per month, against $324 on annual billing for 20 YouCanBookMe Teams members ($360 monthly), because Teams switches to per-member pricing. YouCanBookMe has an ongoing free plan with 1 calendar connection and 1 booking page, while SavvyCal has no ongoing free tier, only a free try and a 30-day money-back guarantee. G2 identifies pricing as SavvyCal's main negative theme, and SavvyCal's monthly base rate is sourced from Capterra.
