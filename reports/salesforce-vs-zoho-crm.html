Salesforce vs Zoho CRM pricing

Salesforce and Zoho CRM both have ongoing free plans and low-priced entry tiers. This page compares them using StackScope's CRM report, last verified October 2026.

Zoho CRM Standard is cheaper than Salesforce Starter Suite on either billing basis: $14, $70 and $280 per month for 1, 5 and 20 users on annual billing, or $20, $100 and $400 billed monthly, against $25, $125 and $500 for Salesforce, which is the same either way. Zoho's free plan covers 3 users and 5,000 total records, while Salesforce's covers 2 users. On AI, Salesforce Starter includes assistive AI (Employee Agent) at no extra charge, with Agentforce consumption sold separately at $500 per 100,000 Flex Credits or $2 per conversation, while Zoho includes up to 30 million hosted-LLM tokens per month and requires Professional or above for its Zia Agents.

FAQ

Common questions

Is Zoho CRM cheaper than Salesforce?

Yes on both billing bases. Zoho CRM Standard is $14, $70 and $280 per month for 1, 5 and 20 users on annual billing ($20, $100 and $400 monthly), versus $25, $125 and $500 for Salesforce Starter Suite.

Which free plan is bigger?

Zoho's covers 3 users and 5,000 total records. Salesforce's covers 2 users.

How do Salesforce and Zoho price AI?

Salesforce Starter includes assistive AI at no extra charge, with Agentforce sold separately at $500 per 100,000 Flex Credits or $2 per conversation. Zoho includes up to 30 million hosted-LLM tokens per month, and Zia Agents require Professional or above.
