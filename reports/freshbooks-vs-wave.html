FreshBooks vs Wave pricing

FreshBooks and Wave both target freelancers and very small businesses. This page compares them using StackScope's accounting report, last verified October 2026.

Wave is cheaper: Starter is $0 and includes unlimited estimates, invoices, bills and bookkeeping records, and Pro is $19 per month or $190 per year, against $23 per month for FreshBooks Lite. The gap grows with team size. FreshBooks includes 1 user and charges $11 per month for each additional Team Member, which StackScope estimates at $67 per month for 5 users, while Wave Pro includes unlimited users at the same price. FreshBooks has no ongoing free plan, only a 30-day trial, and its page still shows a 90%-off promotion that its own offer terms previously said ended August 18, 2026, a conflict StackScope has not resolved. Wave's payments, receipt scanning and payroll are paid add-ons.

FAQ

Common questions

Is Wave cheaper than FreshBooks?

Yes: Wave Starter is $0 and Pro is $19 per month, against $23 per month for FreshBooks Lite. For 5 users StackScope estimates FreshBooks at $67 per month, while Wave Pro stays at $19.

Does FreshBooks charge per user?

It includes 1 user and charges $11 per month for each additional Team Member. Wave Pro includes unlimited users.

Is the FreshBooks promotion real?

Unresolved. The page shows 90% off, but FreshBooks' own offer terms previously said that promotion ended August 18, 2026.
