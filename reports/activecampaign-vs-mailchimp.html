ActiveCampaign vs Mailchimp pricing

ActiveCampaign is known for automation depth and Mailchimp for ease of use and a free plan. This page lines up their published tiers using StackScope's email marketing report, last verified October 2026.

At 2,500 contacts, ActiveCampaign Starter is $39 per month on an annual-equivalent basis against $51 per month for Mailchimp Standard, so ActiveCampaign's entry tier is the cheaper of the two, though Starter caps automation actions at 5 per automation. Mailchimp has an ongoing free plan for 250 contacts and 500 sends per month, while ActiveCampaign has no free plan, only a 14-day trial capped at 100 contacts. The tiers don't line up above that: ActiveCampaign Pro is $375 per month at 10,000 contacts, while Mailchimp Standard at 10,000 contacts is $115 per month, which the vendor presents as a first-year promotional rate. ActiveCampaign's automation depth is its most praised feature.

FAQ

Common questions

Is ActiveCampaign or Mailchimp cheaper?

At 2,500 contacts, ActiveCampaign Starter is $39 per month (annual-equivalent) versus $51 per month for Mailchimp Standard. At 10,000 contacts the comparison isn't like-for-like: ActiveCampaign Pro is $375 per month, while Mailchimp Standard is $115 per month as a first-year promotional rate.

Do ActiveCampaign and Mailchimp have free plans?

Mailchimp does: 250 contacts and 500 sends per month. ActiveCampaign has no free plan, only a 14-day trial capped at 100 contacts.

Which has better automation?

ActiveCampaign's automation depth is consistently its most praised feature, but its Starter tier caps automation actions at 5 per automation. Mailchimp's lower tiers also cap automation.
