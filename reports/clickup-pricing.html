ClickUp pricing 2026

This page covers ClickUp's published prices, free plan limits and the catches, using StackScope's project management software report, last verified October 2026.

ClickUp's free plan has unlimited members, but only 60MB of total shared storage across the whole team, which the report calls the real constraint. Unlimited is $7 per member ($10 billed monthly) and Business is $12 per member ($19 monthly). Business Plus exists but isn't on ClickUp's pricing page; it is visible only after upgrading from inside a workspace. The main catch is that "Unlimited" doesn't mean unlimited: despite the plan name, Goals, time tracking and workload management are gated behind Business. Per ClickUp's own 2026 changelog, the Brain AI add-on is $9 per member per month. ClickUp is praised for deep customization and genuinely all-in-one functionality.
