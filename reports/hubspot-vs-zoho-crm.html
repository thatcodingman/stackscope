HubSpot vs Zoho CRM pricing

HubSpot and Zoho CRM both offer an ongoing free plan and a low-priced entry tier. This page compares them side by side using StackScope's CRM report, last verified October 2026.

On annual billing Zoho CRM Standard is cheaper: $14, $70 and $280 per month for 1, 5 and 20 users, against $20, $100 and $400 for HubSpot Sales Hub Starter. On monthly billing the two are identical, because Zoho Standard is $20, $100 and $400 billed monthly and HubSpot's standard rate is the same either way. HubSpot currently shows a new-customer promotion that brings 5 users to $35 per month, but it is limited-time and not the standing price. Zoho's free plan covers 3 users and 5,000 total records, while HubSpot's covers 2 users and 1,000 contacts. Zoho's catch is a learning curve from its broad feature set, and its Zia Agents require the Professional tier or above.

FAQ

Common questions

Is Zoho CRM cheaper than HubSpot?

On annual billing, yes: Zoho CRM Standard is $14, $70 and $280 per month for 1, 5 and 20 users versus $20, $100 and $400 for HubSpot Sales Hub Starter. Billed monthly, both are $20, $100 and $400.

Which has the bigger free plan, HubSpot or Zoho CRM?

Zoho's covers 3 users and 5,000 total records. HubSpot's covers 2 users and 1,000 contacts. Both are ongoing free plans, not trials.

Does HubSpot have a promotion running?

Yes, a limited-time new-customer rate that brings 5 users to $35 per month. StackScope treats it as promotional, not the standing price of $100 per month.
