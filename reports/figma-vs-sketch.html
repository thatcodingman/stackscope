Figma vs Sketch pricing

Figma and Sketch are the two main UI design tools, and Sketch is cheaper per editor. This page compares them using StackScope's design tools report, last verified October 2026.

Sketch is cheaper per editor: Standard is $12 per month on annual billing ($14 monthly), which comes to $60 for 5 editors and $240 for 20 by StackScope's arithmetic. Figma Professional Full seats are $16 per month ($20 monthly), or $80 for 5 and $320 for 20. The platforms differ more than the prices: Figma runs in the browser with real-time collaboration, while full editing in Sketch requires the Mac app, a real constraint for mixed-OS teams, though Viewers are free and unlimited. Figma has an ongoing free plan with 3 team files and unlimited free Viewers, while Sketch offers only a 30-day trial. Figma's catch is seat-role complexity, since collaborators who need real design editing require a pricier Full seat.

FAQ

Common questions

Is Sketch cheaper than Figma?

Yes: Sketch Standard is $12 per editor per month on annual billing versus $16 for a Figma Professional Full seat, or $60 versus $80 for 5 editors and $240 versus $320 for 20.

Does Sketch work on Windows?

Full editing requires the Mac app, which is a real platform constraint for mixed-OS teams. Viewers are free and unlimited.

Do Figma and Sketch have free plans?

Figma has an ongoing free plan with 3 team files and unlimited free Viewers. Sketch offers a 30-day trial only.
