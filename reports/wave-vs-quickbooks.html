Wave vs QuickBooks Online pricing

Wave's free accounting plan is the usual alternative to QuickBooks for very small businesses. This page compares them using StackScope's accounting report, last verified October 2026.

Wave's Starter plan is $0 and includes unlimited estimates, invoices, bills and bookkeeping records, against $38 per month for QuickBooks Simple Start. Wave's paid Pro tier is $19 per month or $190 per year, priced per business profile with unlimited user access, so 1-user and 5-user prices are identical. QuickBooks Simple Start covers only 1 user plus 2 accountant accesses, and Essentials is $85 per month for 3 users plus 2 accountants. Wave's online payments, receipt scanning and payroll are separate paid add-ons, so the free plan is not the whole cost if you use them. Wave currently runs a $9.50 promotion for the first 3 months of Pro, and Intuit advertises 50% off for 3 months for new customers, so treat both as promotional rather than standing rates.
