Framer pricing 2026

This page covers Framer's published prices, free plan limits and the catches, using StackScope's design tools report, last verified October 2026.

Framer's free plan is ongoing and allows up to 3 editors on a workspace with no active subscription, with 10 CMS collections, 1,000 pages, 5MB uploads, 1 free locale and 1GB of bandwidth; it lacks a custom domain and production publishing. Basic is $10 per month on annual billing ($15 billed monthly) with the first editor included, and includes 30 pages, 2 CMS collections, 1,000 CMS items and 50GB of bandwidth. Each additional Full Editor is $20 per month and content editors are $10 per month. By StackScope's arithmetic from Framer's own rules that comes to $90 per month for 5 editors and $390 per month for 20 editors on annual billing ($95 and $395 monthly). The main catch is that the $10 headline price doesn't represent full multi-editor team cost.

FAQ

Common questions

How much does Framer cost?

Basic is $10 per month on annual billing ($15 billed monthly) with the first editor included. Additional Full Editors are $20 per month each and content editors are $10 per month.

Does Framer have a free plan?

Yes. It is ongoing, with up to 3 editors, 10 CMS collections, 1,000 pages and 1GB of bandwidth, but it lacks a custom domain and production publishing.

What does Framer cost for a team?

By StackScope's arithmetic from Framer's pricing rules: $90 per month for 5 editors and $390 per month for 20 editors on annual billing ($95 and $395 on monthly billing).
