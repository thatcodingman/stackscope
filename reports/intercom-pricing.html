Intercom pricing 2026

This page covers Intercom's published prices, free plan limits and the catches, using StackScope's help desk software report, last verified October 2026.

Intercom has no free plan, only a 14-day trial with no card required. Essential is $29 per seat ($39 billed monthly), Advanced is $85 per seat ($99 monthly) and Expert is $132 per seat ($139 monthly). The main catch is that the seat price isn't the real price: the Fin AI agent bills $0.99 per outcome on top of seats, which is the most repeated complaint across sources, and Copilot adds $29 per agent per month billed annually or $35 monthly. Pro and Proactive Support Plus modules run $99 per month. Intercom gets strong marks for its live-chat interface and Fin's conversational resolution.

How much does Intercom cost per seat?

Essential is $29 per seat ($39 billed monthly), Advanced is $85 ($99 monthly) and Expert is $132 ($139 monthly).

How much does Intercom's Fin AI agent cost?

Fin bills $0.99 per outcome on top of seat prices. This is the most repeated complaint across the sources StackScope reviewed.

Does Intercom have a free plan?

No. There is a 14-day free trial with no card required, but no permanent free plan.
