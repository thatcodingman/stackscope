Buyer guide

How much does payroll software cost for 5 employees?

For 5 employees, Gusto Simple is $79 per month ($49 plus $6 per person) and Justworks Payroll is $90 per month ($50 base plus $8 per employee). Rippling and ADP don't publish self-serve payroll prices, so their cost can't be compared from public data. Gusto Simple is single-state only.

Payroll tools price by employee, so cost grows with headcount. At 20 employees, Gusto Simple is $169 per month and Justworks Payroll is $210. Neither has an ongoing free plan: Gusto's free period ends with the first payroll run, and Justworks advertises 3 months free for Payroll as a promotion.

The cheapest option has limits that matter: Gusto Simple supports one state only, so a team with employees in more than one state needs Plus ($80 plus $12 per person) or Premium ($180 plus $22 per person).

FAQ

Common questions

How much does payroll software cost for 5 employees?

Gusto Simple is $79 per month and Justworks Payroll is $90 per month for 5 employees. Rippling and ADP require a custom quote.

Why don't Rippling and ADP list prices?

Rippling's live pricing page routes payroll configurations to a custom quote, and ADP's Essential, Enhanced and Complete Payroll & HR Plus tiers are quote-only. StackScope doesn't estimate prices that vendors don't publish.

Is there free payroll software?

None of the four has an ongoing free plan. Gusto's free period ends when the first payroll is run, and Justworks and ADP advertise 3 months free as a promotion.
