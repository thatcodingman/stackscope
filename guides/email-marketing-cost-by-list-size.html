Buyer guide

How much does email marketing software cost by list size?

Email marketing is priced by list size, not team size. At 500 contacts Mailchimp is $17 per month and Klaviyo is $20. At 2,500 to 3,000 contacts the published tiers run from $39 (ActiveCampaign Starter) to $51 (Mailchimp). At 10,000 contacts they run from about $115 to $375 per month. Kit's free plan covers up to 10,000 subscribers.

FAQ

Common questions

How much does email marketing software cost for 1,000 contacts?

Kit Creator is $33 per month ($39 billed monthly) and Klaviyo Email is $30 per month at 1,000 profiles. Mailchimp's published tiers are 500 and 2,500 contacts, at $17 and $51 per month.

What is the cheapest email marketing software at 10,000 contacts?

Mailchimp lists $115 per month, though as a first-year promotional rate, and Kit about $117 per month. Klaviyo is $150 and ActiveCampaign Pro is $375.

Is there free email marketing software?

Yes. Kit's free plan covers up to 10,000 subscribers but withholds automation. Mailchimp's free plan covers 250 contacts and 500 sends per month, and Klaviyo's covers 250 active profiles and 500 sends per month.
