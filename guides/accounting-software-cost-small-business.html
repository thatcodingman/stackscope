Buyer guide

How much does accounting software cost for a small business?

Entry plans range from free (Wave Starter) to $38 per month (QuickBooks Online Simple Start). FreshBooks Lite is $23 per month and Xero Early is $27 per month after its October 1, 2026 increase. QuickBooks limits Simple Start to 1 user plus 2 accountant accesses, so a 5-user team needs Plus at $140 per month. Wave's low price comes with payment processing fees.

Only Wave has an ongoing free plan. QuickBooks Online, Xero and FreshBooks each have a 30-day free trial. Intuit currently advertises 50% off for 3 months for new QuickBooks customers, and FreshBooks' pricing page still shows a 90%-off promotion that its own offer terms previously said ended August 18, 2026.

User and client limits drive the real cost. QuickBooks Essentials supports 3 users and Plus supports 5, FreshBooks Plus covers 50 billable clients, and Xero includes accountant and bookkeeper access at no extra cost.

FAQ

Common questions

How much does accounting software cost for a small business?

From free for Wave Starter, to $23 per month for FreshBooks Lite, $27 for Xero Early and $38 for QuickBooks Online Simple Start, at standard prices.

Is there free accounting software?

Wave's Starter plan is $0 and ongoing, with unlimited invoices, estimates, bills and bookkeeping records. Its cost comes from payment processing fees and paid add-ons. QuickBooks Online, Xero and FreshBooks offer 30-day free trials only.

Did accounting software prices go up in 2026?

Xero did. Effective October 1, 2026, Early rose from $25 to $27 per month, Growing from $55 to $59 and Established from $90 to $97.
