Buyer guide

How much does help desk software cost for 5 agents?

At listed entry-tier prices a 5-agent team pays about $95 per month for Zendesk or Freshdesk (5 agents at $19), $125 for Help Scout (5 at $25) and $145 for Intercom (5 at $29). Help Scout's free plan supports up to 5 users but is limited to 100 contacts per month and 1 inbox, and Freshdesk's free plan covers 2 agents.

FAQ

Common questions

How much does help desk software cost for 5 agents?

At listed entry-tier prices, about $95 per month for Zendesk or Freshdesk, $125 for Help Scout and $145 for Intercom, calculated as five agents at each tool's per-agent price.

Is there a free help desk for 5 agents?

Help Scout's free plan supports up to 5 users, with 100 contacts per month and 1 inbox. Freshdesk's free plan covers up to 2 agents. Zendesk and Intercom have no free plan.

Do help desks charge extra for AI?

Most do. Zendesk and Intercom bill AI per resolution or outcome on top of seat prices, and Help Scout charges $0.75 per resolution for AI Answers after a 3-month trial.
