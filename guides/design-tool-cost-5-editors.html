Buyer guide

How much do design tools cost for 5 editors?

On annual billing, 5 editors cost $60 per month on Sketch, $80 on Figma, about $90 on Framer and $1,250 per year (about $104 per month) on Canva Business. Sketch editing requires the Mac app, and Canva's cheaper Pro plan is one-person only, so a Canva team needs Business.

These tools aren't direct substitutes. Figma and Sketch are interface design tools, Framer publishes sites, and Canva is a template-driven design tool, so price should be weighed against what each does.

Free plans differ: Figma's includes 3 team files and unlimited free Viewers, Framer's allows up to 3 editors but has no custom domain or production publishing, Canva's is a one-person plan, and Sketch offers only a 30-day trial.

FAQ

Common questions

How much do design tools cost for 5 editors?

On annual billing about $60 per month for Sketch, $80 for Figma, $90 for Framer and $1,250 per year for Canva Business, which is about $104 per month.

Can a team use Canva Pro?

No. Canva Pro is a one-person plan regardless of price. A team needs Canva Business at $250 per person per year, and Canva Teams is no longer available for new sign-ups.

Is there a free design tool for a team?

Figma's free plan includes 3 team files and unlimited free Viewers. Framer's free plan allows up to 3 editors but lacks a custom domain and production publishing. Sketch has no free editing plan, only a 30-day trial.
