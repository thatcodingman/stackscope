Buyer guide

How much does e-signature software cost for 5 users?

On annual billing, 5 users cost $40 per month on SignNow Business, about $87.50 on Dropbox Sign Standard (5 at $17.50), $95 on PandaDoc Starter and about $125 on DocuSign Standard (5 at $25). The cheapest isn't automatically the best: SignNow's own pages disagree on whether it caps envelopes, and DocuSign Standard allows 100 envelopes per user per year.

FAQ

Common questions

How much does e-signature software cost for 5 users?

On annual billing about $40 per month for SignNow Business, $87.50 for Dropbox Sign Standard, $95 for PandaDoc Starter and $125 for DocuSign Standard. The Dropbox Sign and DocuSign totals are five users at the per-user price.

Is there a free e-signature tool for a team?

PandaDoc's free plan allows unlimited seats but is capped at 60 documents a year and 2 recipients per document. Dropbox Sign's free plan allows 3 signature requests per month. DocuSign's free plan is for 1 user only. SignNow has only a 7-day trial.

Do e-signature tools limit how many documents you can send?

Some do. DocuSign Standard allows 100 envelopes per user per year, and SignNow's own pages disagree on whether it caps envelopes. PandaDoc Starter has no monthly send cap.
