Buyer guide

How much does project management software cost for 5 people?

At listed entry-tier prices on annual billing, 5 members cost about $35 per month on ClickUp (5 at $7), $45 on Monday.com (5 at $9), $50 on Notion (5 at $10) and $54.95 on Asana (5 at $10.99). Only ClickUp's free plan allows more than 2 members, but it caps total shared storage at 60MB.

The cheapest entry tier isn't always the one you'll need: ClickUp's Unlimited plan gates Goals, time tracking and workload management behind Business at $12 per member, which would be $60 per month for 5.

The free plans are limited in different ways. ClickUp's has unlimited members but only 60MB of total shared storage. Asana's free plan is limited to 2 users, Monday.com's to 2 seats and 3 boards, and Notion's collaborative workspaces cap at 1,000 blocks.

FAQ

Common questions

How much does project management software cost for 5 people?

At listed entry-tier prices on annual billing, about $35 per month for ClickUp, $45 for Monday.com, $50 for Notion and $54.95 for Asana, calculated as five members at each tool's per-member price.

Is there a free project management tool for a team of 5?

ClickUp's free plan allows unlimited members but only 60MB of total shared storage. Asana's free plan covers 2 users and Monday.com's covers 2 seats with 3 boards. Notion's free plan caps collaborative workspaces at 1,000 blocks.

Does Monday.com's 3-seat minimum matter for 5 people?

Not for 5. The minimum means every paid plan starts at 3 seats, which is $27 per month on Basic with annual billing, so it only matters for teams of 1 or 2.
